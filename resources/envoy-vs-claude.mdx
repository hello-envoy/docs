Keep **Claude** for long-form writing, careful drafts, and work grounded in documents you upload. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Claude. Most people keep both.

You can run the project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book.

## Claude vs Envoy

Claude is where you write and reason from a conversation, often with your own files beside it. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Claude for** long documents, careful rewrites, and chats grounded in files you add to a Project.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Claude's writing, Artifacts, or coding help in Claude Code. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Claude is

Claude is Anthropic's assistant. You talk to it at claude.ai, in the desktop app, or in the tools Anthropic ships for longer work. People use it when the answer needs to stay accurate across a long draft or a stack of source files.

### Chat and long-form writing

Claude's everyday surface is a conversation. You can ask it to explain, compare options, draft, or revise. It is especially strong when the piece is long: a brief, a scope note, a letter, or a plan you want rewritten with a steadier tone. You stay in the chat and steer the next pass.

### Projects and Artifacts

**Projects** group chats with a shared knowledge base. You can upload documents, text, or code, and add instructions so Claude answers with that material in mind. Free accounts can create projects, with a smaller cap than paid plans. Paid plans extend how much material a project can draw on.

**Artifacts** put a longer result — a document, a diagram, a preview — in a window next to the chat so you can read and revise it apart from the back-and-forth. That is a writing and building surface, not a directory of local service providers.

### Coding, in a different surface

**Claude Code** is Anthropic's coding tool. It runs in the terminal, reads a codebase, and edits under your review. Use it for software. It is not how you search Envoy for a florist or an electrician.

**Claude is for** careful writing, work grounded in documents you provide, and — in Claude Code — software tasks.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

Claude can stay open for the long draft. Envoy holds the shortlist, the shared details, and the email.

A planner can run the project for someone else: add the Contacts, keep the location, budget, and deadline on the project, and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project. A Claude Project your team shares is a different product, with its own sharing rules.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search you do before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. A Claude Project full of notes is not Envoy's contact list, and Envoy does not import it.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**. A contractor named in a Claude draft is added by you.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Ask what a venue should hear first, or what the caterer needs before they quote. When planning is complete, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for the contacts on the project. You still review every send.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a Charleston weekend wedding, about 120 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date you need answers. You wrote the scope in Claude from the notes you uploaded. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the photographer include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send**. You still review every send. The reply shows in your inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

A kitchen remodel uses the same three tabs — cabinets, counters, an electrician, etc.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Claude is built for **writing and reasoning from what you give it**. Paste a neighborhood, a scope, or a PDF, and it can draft the questions. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up.

Use Claude for the long brief. Use Envoy to name the people on the project and email them.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date and one brief. On a wedding, that is the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc. A remodel works the same way — an electrician, a plumber, a cabinet maker, etc.

In Claude, you can keep that material in a Project and write a master brief or an Artifact. Teammates on an eligible Claude plan can share a Project. That sharing is inside Claude. It is not an Envoy login, and it does not send from your Envoy inbox.

On Envoy, those businesses are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. One person sends. The couple can brainstorm together in Claude, and they can also brainstorm in Envoy **Chat** on this project's saved details. Only Envoy outreach sends from the one connected inbox.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Claude can **write the note**. You copy it into mail, or you use it as the reference while Envoy drafts from the project. Claude does not file the reply on an Envoy **Outreach** thread.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact you picked. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to **Outreach**. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Claude has its own account, including free and paid plans for projects and other features. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical week:** write the scope in Claude, browse wedding vendors on The Knot if it is a wedding, and shortlist plus email on Envoy. Contracts stay with each business.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Claude for long-form writing and for documents you want in the chat. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Claude's row is the long draft grounded in your files. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email.

| Category | Envoy | Claude | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-form writing from your documents | **Chat** uses the details saved on this project, then outreach drafts | Projects keep files and instructions with the chat. Artifacts hold a longer piece beside the conversation | **Claude** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed. They are not reviews | Drafts questions from what you upload. It does not return Envoy's contact list | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in | You can ask for the wording, then send it from your own mail | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | A Claude Project holds documents and chats. It is not a service-provider shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | You can draft in Claude before you open Envoy. That draft is not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Create draft**, the **Draft with Envoy** bar if you want a rewrite, and **Approve & Send** | A careful brief or a list of questions in the chat | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A name in the draft stays in Claude until you add it on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, before **Send reply** | The Claude thread, unless you sent the note from your own mail | **Envoy** |
