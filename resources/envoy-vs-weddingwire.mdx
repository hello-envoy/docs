WeddingWire is a wedding directory where couples search, compare, and contact local professionals — venues, photographers, florists, caterers, and more. It is part of **The Knot Worldwide**, the same company as [The Knot](/resources/envoy-vs-the-knot), so the two directories overlap. Envoy takes a different role: a **project workspace** where you choose contacts and **send outreach from your own inbox**.

Here is how to tell them apart.

## At a glance

| | **Envoy** | **WeddingWire** |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Directory of wedding professionals |
| **Company context** | Envoy, for people running a project | Part of The Knot Worldwide, with The Knot |
| **Who initiates contact** | You choose contacts and email them | You search the directory and request information from listings |
| **Best for** | Several wedding contacts on one project, with shared details | Comparing local professionals, reviews, and pricing information |
| **Project context** | Title, location, budget, deadline, and contacts together | A listing and inquiry for each professional you contact |
| **Communication** | Email from your Gmail or Outlook | Messages and quote requests on WeddingWire |
| **Planning help** | Project **Chat** and **Revise with Envoy** | Planning tools, inspiration, and a reviews community |
| **Search before signup** | Yes — homepage contact search | You can browse public listings |

## What WeddingWire does well

WeddingWire is built for **discovery inside a wedding directory**:

- Search and compare local wedding professionals in one catalog
- Couple reviews, photos, and pricing or availability details on listings
- A familiar path from "who is nearby?" to "request a quote"
- Listed businesses pay for premium placement and leads so couples can find them

If you want a dedicated wedding directory — and you are fine requesting information through that marketplace — WeddingWire is a natural first stop. Because it shares a parent company with The Knot, you may recognize many of the same businesses there.

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy is where the wedding stays **organized after you know who you might hire**.

### A shortlist you control

Describe the wedding on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) and search by location before you sign up. You pick the contacts. Badges such as **Onboarded to Envoy** and **Unverified listing** tell you how each result is listed. Later, you can search again from **Dashboard** during project creation.

### The whole wedding stays on one project

WeddingWire inquiries are tied to individual listings. Envoy attaches the venue, photographer, florist, caterer, and anyone else to **one project**, with the same location, budget, and deadline. Open **Overview** for the details, **Chat** to plan, and **Outreach** for the emails.

### You send the email

Envoy drafts outreach from your project details. You review it, **Revise with Envoy** if the tone or the missing details need a pass, and send from Gmail or Outlook. The reply lands in your inbox, on the thread you started.

### One brief, reused

**Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to know before you write a separate version of the same wedding for every person. When you finish planning in **Chat**, Envoy prepares outreach drafts for the contacts on the project.

## When to use WeddingWire

Choose **WeddingWire** when:

- You want a wedding-specific directory with reviews and listing details
- Comparing professionals in a category is the main task right now
- You prefer to request information through the marketplace
- You are already using The Knot and want the sibling directory in the same company

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- The wedding needs **more than one contact**, and they should share one set of details
- You want to **start the conversation**, with a message you approve
- Follow-up belongs in **your email**, next to the rest of your planning
- You want project **Chat** to shape questions before the first send
- You want to evaluate contacts **before creating an account**

## Can you use both?

Yes. Use WeddingWire to research professionals and read reviews. Use Envoy to **keep the shortlist, the plan, and the outreach** together. The directories overlap with The Knot; Envoy does not replace either directory, and it does not build a guest list or a wedding website.

<Tip>
  Search for wedding contacts on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you sign up, then keep the ones you choose on a single project.
</Tip>
