**Plan any project. We line up the contacts.**

Most home and business projects stall in the messy middle — when you're trying to find the right people, keep details straight, and actually get responses. Envoy is built for that moment: contacts, project details, and outreach. You find service providers (called **Contacts**), save the location, budget, and deadline on one project, and send outreach from your own inbox. That fits homeowners, small businesses, and anyone else coordinating more than one service provider.

## Weddings are a perfect fit

A wedding is an especially strong use of that same workspace. You are coordinating several vendors at once — the florist, the caterer, the photographer, the venue, and others — who share one date and one set of details. Envoy holds that shortlist and the email on the project.

<Note>
  Most couples keep both. Envoy finds service providers (**Contacts**) and helps you email them. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, review directories, contracts and invoices, and day-of tools stay in the products you already use for those jobs.
</Note>

## The problem with "post and wait"

Traditional marketplaces ask you to submit a request and wait for pros to come to you. That works for a simple one-off job. It breaks down when:

- You're coordinating **several service providers** for one project — a wedding's florist, caterer, photographer, venue, and other service providers, or the pros on a renovation or a small-business fit-out
- You want to **choose who you contact**, not whoever responds to a posted request
- You need the **same details** across weeks of planning and follow-up
- You want replies in **your email**, not buried in a platform inbox

Envoy flips the model. You discover contacts, build your shortlist, plan the work, and reach out on your terms.

## How Envoy fits your workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Search by project description and location on the homepage before you sign up, or during project creation from **Dashboard**. A wedding search might be a florist, caterer, photographer, venue, or another service provider. Review contacts with clear badges like **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize">
    Create a project, attach contacts, and capture the details contacts actually need — location, budget, and deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan">
    Use **Chat** to refine scope and decide what to ask before you send the first email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach out">
    Draft, revise, and send outreach from your connected inbox. Follow up in the same thread when replies arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who Envoy is for

**Couples** can run their own project. **Planners, friends, and family** can coordinate a consumer project for someone else and send outreach from the inbox they connect. A wedding — florist, caterer, photographer, venue, and more — is a perfect fit.

**Homeowners and renters** planning renovations, repairs, or upgrades who want to run the process themselves.

**Small business owners** opening locations, upgrading facilities, or coordinating service providers for an event or launch.

**Anyone coordinating several service providers** who wants the brief, the contacts, and the outreach on one project.

If you're a **service provider** looking to receive project inquiries through Envoy, see [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro).
