**Coordinate the wedding vendors. Keep the contacts, the details, and the outreach together.**

A wedding asks you to line up several vendors at once: the florist, the caterer, the photographer, and the venue. Envoy is where you find those service providers (called **Contacts**), keep the wedding's details on one project, and send outreach from your own inbox.

Homeowners and small businesses can use the same workspace when a renovation or a fit-out needs more than one service provider.

<Note>
  Most couples keep both. Envoy finds wedding vendors and helps you email them. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, review directories, contracts and invoices, and the wedding-day plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.
</Note>

## The problem with "post and wait"

Traditional marketplaces ask you to submit a request and wait for pros to come to you. That works for a simple one-off job. It breaks down when:

- You're coordinating **several wedding vendors** — florist, caterer, photographer, venue — for one day
- You want to **choose who you contact**, not whoever responds to a posted request
- You need the **same details** across weeks of planning and follow-up
- You want replies in **your email**, not buried in a platform inbox

A renovation or a small-business project with more than one service provider runs into the same gap.

Envoy flips the model. You discover contacts, build your shortlist, plan the work, and reach out on your terms.

## How Envoy fits your workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Search by project description and location on the homepage before you sign up, or during project creation from **Dashboard**. For a wedding, that might be a florist, caterer, photographer, or venue. Review contacts with clear badges like **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize">
    Create a project, attach contacts, and capture the details contacts actually need — location, budget, and deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan">
    Use **Chat** to refine scope and decide what to ask before you send the first email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach out">
    Draft, revise, and send outreach from your connected inbox. Follow up in the same thread when replies arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who Envoy is for

**Couples** coordinating several wedding vendors — florist, caterer, photographer, venue — who want the contacts, the details, and the outreach on one project.

**Planners, friends, and family** coordinating a consumer project for someone else. You run the project and send outreach from the inbox you connect.

**Homeowners, small businesses, and anyone else** lining up more than one service provider — a renovation, a fit-out, or another multi-vendor job.

If you're a **service provider** looking to receive project inquiries through Envoy, see [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro).
