Zola is a modern wedding planning suite. Couples use it for a **registry**, a **wedding website**, guest tools, and a marketplace of wedding professionals. Envoy does not replace that suite. Envoy is where you **coordinate the wedding as a project** and **email contacts from your own inbox**.

This page separates those jobs so you can use each product for what it is good at.

## At a glance

| | **Envoy** | **Zola** |
|---|-----------|----------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Registry, website, guest tools, and a marketplace |
| **Best known for** | Choosing contacts and emailing them about one project | Registry and a clean planning experience for couples and guests |
| **Who initiates contact** | You choose contacts and send the email | You browse Zola's marketplace and plan inside Zola |
| **Best for** | Venue, florist, photographer, caterer, and others on one project | Gifts, the wedding website, guests, and in-product planning |
| **Project context** | Location, budget, deadline, and contacts in the workspace | Website, registry, checklist, and guest tools in one suite |
| **Communication** | Email from your Gmail or Outlook | Zola's own planning and marketplace experience |
| **Planning help** | Project **Chat** and **Revise with Envoy** | Checklists, budget tools, and registry guidance |
| **Search before signup** | Yes — homepage contact search | Core planning features are part of a Zola account |

## What Zola does well

Zola is a good home for the **couple-and-guest side** of a wedding:

- A registry that covers products, experiences, and cash funds, tied to the wedding website
- A modern website and invitations flow guests actually use
- Guest list and RSVP tools in the same place as the registry
- A marketplace where you can look for venues, photographers, caterers, and similar professionals
- Planning extras such as a checklist and a budget, in a polished interface

If the question is "where do guests go, and where do gifts live?", Zola is built for that.

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy is built for **lining up the people who will do the work** and writing to them with the wedding's real details.

### Contacts live on the project

Search on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you sign up, or from **Dashboard** when you create the project. You choose who to add. A wedding project can hold the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, and the rest as **Contacts**, with badges such as **Onboarded to Envoy** so you know how each listing is presented.

### Outreach with the brief attached

Location, budget, and deadline live on the project. Envoy uses that context to draft emails. You edit, use **Revise with Envoy**, and send from Gmail or Outlook with **Approve & Send**. Replies stay on that thread in **Outreach**.

### Chat that knows this wedding

The **Chat** tab answers planning questions for this project — what to ask a caterer, what to send a venue first, what to prioritize if the date is close. It is scoped to your title, description, location, budget, and deadline.

### A clear split of jobs

Zola's marketplace can help you discover professionals. Envoy is the place you **run the outreach** once you have people to contact. Envoy does not build your registry, your wedding website, or your RSVP list.

## When to use Zola

Choose **Zola** when:

- The registry is the center of what you need
- You want a wedding website and guest tools in the same product
- You like planning checklists and a guest experience in one suite
- You want to browse a wedding marketplace inside that same account

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- You need to **contact several service providers** about the same wedding
- You want one project for the details those contacts need
- You want to **approve every email** and send it from your own inbox
- Follow-up should live with you, in **Outreach**, as replies arrive
- You want to look for contacts **before you create an account**

## Can you use both?

Yes — and for many weddings that is the practical split. Keep the registry, website, and guest list on Zola. Use Envoy to **find contacts, plan the ask, and send the emails**. Neither product has to do the other's job.

<Tip>
  When you are ready to contact people, describe the wedding on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) and start a shortlist. Your registry and website can stay where they are.
</Tip>
