Keep **Zola** for guests and gifts, and use **Envoy** when it is time to email vendors. Planners already run a registry and a website in one product and a separate inbox for the florist and the venue. Nothing here asks you to move the registry.

**Contacts** (your vendors / service providers) are the businesses you hire. In Envoy they live on the wedding project. They are not your guest list and not a personal address book.

Zola remains the couple suite: registry, wedding website, and guest tools. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) shows that split. Envoy does not try to be the site guests open.

## What Zola is

Zola is a modern wedding planning product aimed at the couple and their guests. People know it first for the **registry**. The rest of the suite is built so the website, the guest list, and the gifts stay connected.

### Registry

The registry covers products, experiences, and cash funds, and it links to the wedding website so guests have one place to go. For many couples this is the reason they create a Zola account. Managing gifts, group gifts, and cash in one registry is the job Zola is known for doing well.

### Wedding website and invitations

Zola includes a wedding website and invitations, so the public version of the wedding — schedule, travel, registry link — can live next to the gifts. Guests get a single link. You are not maintaining a separate site by hand.

### Guest list and RSVPs

Guest list tools and RSVPs sit in the same account as the registry and the site. You can see who was invited and who has responded without exporting a spreadsheet from a different app. That is couple-and-guest planning, and it is a core part of the suite.

### Checklist, budget, and vendor marketplace

Zola also includes planning extras: a checklist, a budget, and a marketplace where you can look for venues, photographers, caterers, and similar professionals, with filters such as location. The marketplace is real, and it is convenient because it shares an account with the registry. It is one feature inside a suite whose center is still gifts, the website, and guests.

**Zola is for** couples who want an all-in-one home for the registry, the wedding website, and guest tools, with vendor browsing available in that same product.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **coordinating vendors** and emailing them. Those businesses are **Contacts** on a project. Leave Zola's registry, website, invitations, and RSVPs where guests already are.

A planner coordinating for the couple can be the person who adds Contacts and hits send. The guest-facing suite can stay in the couple's Zola account.

### Project workspace

A wedding project has three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Location, budget, deadline, and every contact on this wedding |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy before you write |
| **Outreach** | Draft, revise, send, and reply |

The details contacts need — where the wedding is, what you can spend, when you need an answer — live on the project, not only on a public website.

### Contacts and discovery

Search from the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you sign up. Describe the wedding and add a ZIP or postal code. You can take up to **8 contacts** into account creation. That limit is the pre-signup shortlist only. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project — including vendors who never appeared in search.

A florist you already booked and a caterer you just found can sit on the same project. Click **+ New contact** and enter name and email, or **+ Attach existing** on **Overview** if they are already in your directory. See [Find and add contacts](/help-center/contacts/find-and-add-contacts).

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business has confirmed its listing, so you have a clearer reason to trust the card. **Unverified listing** means a consumer added or manages it and the business has not confirmed it. You can still write to them; double-check the email. Badges do not tell you when they will reply. More on [badges](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

If search is thin for your city, that is a coverage limit of the query, not a reason to abandon the florist you already have. Add them directly. Zola's marketplace can still be where you browse.

### Chat

**Chat** uses this project's title, description, location, budget, and deadline. Use it to decide what a venue needs versus what a photographer needs, and what can wait until the second email. Completed planning can generate outreach drafts for the contacts you attached.

### A pass through one wedding

**Overview:** city, budget, reply-by date, and the caterer you added by email because you already loved them. **Chat:** "What should we ask before we talk menu?" **Outreach:** draft, **Revise with Envoy**, **Approve & Send**. Their answer is in your inbox and on **Outreach**. The registry link stays on Zola.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It drafts, sends what you approve, and shows those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect from **Account** whenever you want.

Connect Gmail or Outlook under **Connected Email Accounts**. **Approve & Send** delivers from your address.

**Envoy is for** the couple or their planner when several vendors need the same private brief. Guests stay on Zola.

## Registry, website, and guest tools

This is Zola's home ground. A guest can open the website, RSVP, and buy from the registry without you forwarding three different links. Invitations and the guest list sit in that flow. Hand guests Zola. Hand vendors an email from Envoy.

If the decision is "where does the couple planning suite live?", the answer is Zola.

## Finding vendors and choosing contacts

Zola's marketplace lets you **look for wedding vendors** in the same account as the registry. That is convenient when you want to stay in one login while you browse.

Envoy is the next step: **put the vendors on a project and email them**, whether they came from Zola's marketplace, a Knot list, or your own notes. Homepage search is available before an account. The registry does not have to move.

Couples who already use Zola for gifts can run vendor outreach in Envoy. The marketplace and the inbox do different work.

## How you plan the work and write to people

Zola's checklist and budget help you track wedding planning inside the suite. They are general planning tools next to the website and the registry.

Envoy's planning is **scoped to the project you will email about**. On **Chat**, you work through questions for the contacts on the wedding. On **Outreach**, the draft already knows the location, budget, and deadline. **Revise with Envoy** is for the message itself — tone, length, missing details — before you send from Gmail or Outlook.

Follow-up stays in your inbox. Zola remains the place guests visit. Your caterer thread does not need to live in the registry.

## What you use each product for day to day

- **Zola** holds the registry, the wedding website, invitations, the guest list, and RSVPs.
- **Envoy** holds the vendors, the private project details, and the emails.
- Zola's marketplace can still help you look. Envoy is where you write, including to people you add by name.

Homepage search is free before you sign up. After you sign in with Google or Outlook, account details are shown in the product. Browsing is free for couples on Zola too. When a vendor email goes out from Envoy, that message is yours.

<Note>
  **A typical planning week:** registry and guest site on Zola, RSVPs on Zola or Joy, vendor browsing on The Knot or WeddingWire if you want volume, shortlist and email on Envoy, the photographer's contract in their own system (often HoneyBook).
</Note>

## Conclusion

Leave the registry, website, and guest list on Zola. Use Envoy when the work is emailing vendors about this wedding. The matrix gives Zola the suite guests actually use, and gives Envoy the project and the inbox.

### Comparison matrix

Zola's row is the registry and wedding website. Envoy's rows are about vendors and email, not about replacing guest tools.

| Category | Envoy | Zola | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private brief for every vendor | Location, budget, and deadline on one project, reused in each draft | Checklist and budget live beside the public website and registry | **Envoy** |
| Registry, website, and guests | Vendor email only. Guests belong on a site built for them | Registry, wedding website, invitations, guest list, and RSVPs in one suite | **Zola** |
| Email a vendor you already like | Add them with name and email, or attach them from your directory, then send | Browse Zola's marketplace in the same account as the registry | **Envoy** |
| The message guests never see | **Revise with Envoy**, then send from Gmail or Outlook after you approve | Guest-facing pages and registry activity | **Envoy** |
| Questions before you write the caterer | **Chat** on this wedding's saved details | The couple's overall checklist | **Envoy** |
| Start without moving the registry | Free homepage search. Up to 8 contacts before sign-in; add more after, registry untouched | The site and registry start in a Zola account | **Envoy** |
| Vendor replies | Your inbox and **Outreach**. Guest RSVPs stay on Zola | RSVPs and gift activity on Zola | **Envoy** |

<Tip>
  Leave the registry and wedding website on Zola. When a vendor needs a real email, add them on Envoy — search first if you want, or type in someone you already know.
</Tip>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can my planner run Envoy while we keep Zola?">
    Yes. The planner can own the Envoy project and the inbox they send from. The couple can keep the Zola registry and website. They are separate logins on purpose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can we keep Zola, The Knot, and Envoy?">
    Yes. Zola for guests and gifts, The Knot for browsing and reviews, Envoy for the vendors you email. Add Knot or Zola finds into Envoy yourself with **+ New contact** or search. There is no automatic import.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to replies?">
    Vendor replies come to the Gmail or Outlook account that sent them, and the thread is on **Outreach**. Guest RSVPs stay on Zola. Envoy only handles email related to the contacts you selected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
