**Envoy vs Zola** splits wedding planning into two products that are good at different halves of it. Zola is a couple planning suite: registry, wedding website, guest tools, and a marketplace. Envoy is a project workspace for **Contacts** and vendor outreach from your own email.

If you need gifts and a site guests will open, start with Zola. If you need to find wedding vendors and write to them with one shared brief, this page — and the [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) at the end — shows where Envoy fits.

## What Zola is

Zola is a modern wedding planning product aimed at the couple and their guests. People know it first for the **registry**. The rest of the suite is built so the website, the guest list, and the gifts stay connected.

### Registry

The registry covers products, experiences, and cash funds, and it links to the wedding website so guests have one place to go. For many couples this is the reason they create a Zola account. Managing gifts, group gifts, and cash in one registry is the job Zola is known for doing well.

### Wedding website and invitations

Zola includes a wedding website and invitations, so the public version of the wedding — schedule, travel, registry link — can live next to the gifts. Guests get a single link. You are not maintaining a separate site by hand.

### Guest list and RSVPs

Guest list tools and RSVPs sit in the same account as the registry and the site. You can see who was invited and who has responded without exporting a spreadsheet from a different app. That is couple-and-guest planning, and it is a core part of the suite.

### Checklist, budget, and vendor marketplace

Zola also includes planning extras: a checklist, a budget, and a marketplace where you can look for venues, photographers, caterers, and similar professionals, with filters such as location. The marketplace is real, and it is convenient because it shares an account with the registry. It is one feature inside a suite whose center is still gifts, the website, and guests.

**Zola is for** couples who want an all-in-one home for the registry, the wedding website, and guest tools, with vendor browsing available in that same product.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **coordinating the service providers** on the wedding and emailing them yourself. Those providers are **Contacts** on a project. Envoy will not replace Zola's registry, website, invitations, or RSVPs. Leave that suite where guests already are, and use Envoy for vendor outreach.

### Project workspace

A wedding project has three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Location, budget, deadline, and every contact on this wedding |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy before you write |
| **Outreach** | Draft, revise, send, and reply |

The details contacts need — where the wedding is, what you can spend, when you need an answer — live on the project, not only on a public website.

### Contacts and discovery

Search from the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you sign up. Describe the wedding, add a ZIP or postal code, and review results with badges such as **Onboarded to Envoy**. Select up to 8 contacts to bring into account creation. After you sign in, search from **Dashboard** while you create the project or add contacts later.

You choose the shortlist. A florist you already know and a caterer you just found can sit on the same project.

### Chat

**Chat** uses this project's title, description, location, budget, and deadline. Use it to decide what a venue needs versus what a photographer needs, and what can wait until the second email. Completed planning can generate outreach drafts for the contacts you attached.

### Outreach from your inbox

Connect Gmail or Outlook under **Connected Email Accounts**. **Revise with Envoy** when you want the draft shorter or more specific. **Approve & Send** delivers it from your address. Replies come back to you and show on the **Outreach** tab.

**Envoy is for** anyone running the wedding project who needs to contact several vendors with the same facts and keep the threads.

## Registry, website, and guest tools

This is Zola's home ground. A guest can open the website, RSVP, and buy from the registry without you forwarding three different links. Invitations and the guest list sit in that flow. Envoy has no equivalent, and it should not be the product you hand to guests.

If the decision is "where does the couple planning suite live?", the answer is Zola.

## Finding vendors and choosing contacts

Zola's marketplace lets you **look for wedding vendors** in the same account as the registry. That is convenient when you want to stay in one login while you browse.

Envoy is built around the next step: **choosing contacts and preparing to email them**. Homepage search is available before you create an account, and the people you select become part of the project. You are not asked to move your registry in order to start a shortlist.

Couples who already love Zola for gifts can still run vendor outreach in Envoy. The marketplace and the inbox do different work.

## How you plan the work and write to people

Zola's checklist and budget help you track wedding planning inside the suite. They are general planning tools next to the website and the registry.

Envoy's planning is **scoped to the project you will email about**. On **Chat**, you work through questions for the contacts on the wedding. On **Outreach**, the draft already knows the location, budget, and deadline. **Revise with Envoy** is for the message itself — tone, length, missing details — before you send from Gmail or Outlook.

Follow-up stays in your inbox. Zola remains the place guests visit. Your caterer thread does not need to live in the registry.

## What you use each product for day to day

A practical split looks like this:

- **Zola** holds the registry, the wedding website, invitations, the guest list, and RSVPs.
- **Envoy** holds the contacts, the private project details, and the emails to vendors.
- Zola's marketplace can still help you discover professionals. Envoy is where you write to the ones you choose.

You do not have to pick one login for the whole wedding.

## Conclusion

**Zola** wins the all-in-one couple suite: registry, wedding website, and guest tools in a product guests can actually use. **Envoy** wins the vendor-outreach side of wedding planning: a multi-contact project, contacts you choose, and email from your own inbox.

Keep the registry and the site on Zola. Run the shortlist and the vendor emails in Envoy.

### Comparison matrix

Most rows below favor Envoy because they are about contacting service providers. Exactly one row favors Zola: the registry and wedding website suite, which is the job Zola is built for.

| Category | Envoy | Zola | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-contact project workspace | One project shares location, budget, and deadline across every contact you will email | Checklist, budget, and vendor browsing beside the registry and website | **Envoy** |
| Registry and wedding website | A workspace for contacts and outreach; guests need a site and a registry elsewhere | Registry, wedding website, invitations, guest list, and RSVPs in one couple suite | **Zola** |
| You choose who to contact | You select contacts and decide who receives a message | You can browse Zola's marketplace from the same account as your registry | **Envoy** |
| Outreach from your own email | Draft, **Revise with Envoy**, and send from Gmail or Outlook after you approve | Planning and marketplace activity stay in Zola | **Envoy** |
| Project-scoped planning | **Chat** is limited to this wedding and the details you saved | Checklist and budget for the couple's overall plan | **Envoy** |
| Contact shortlist before signup | Homepage search and up to 8 contacts before an account | The registry, site, and guest tools start with a Zola account | **Envoy** |
| Follow-up in your inbox | Vendor replies in your email and on **Outreach** | Guest and registry activity stay on Zola | **Envoy** |

<Tip>
  When you are ready to contact people, describe the wedding on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) and start a shortlist. Leave the registry and wedding website on Zola.
</Tip>
