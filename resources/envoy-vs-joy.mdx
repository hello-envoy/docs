Joy (withjoy.com) is a wedding website and guest-planning product. Couples use it for the **site guests visit**, **RSVPs**, and the guest list. Envoy is for a different slice of the same wedding: **finding contacts and emailing them** — venue, florist, photographer, caterer — from a project workspace and your own inbox.

Joy is lighter on that service-provider work. This page shows where each one belongs.

## At a glance

| | **Envoy** | **Joy** |
|---|-----------|---------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Wedding website, RSVPs, and guest planning |
| **Center of gravity** | The people you need to hire and the emails you send them | The experience guests see and how they respond |
| **Who initiates contact** | You choose contacts and email them | You manage guests and the details you publish to them |
| **Best for** | Multi-contact wedding projects and follow-up | A site, RSVPs, and a guest list guests can use |
| **Project context** | Location, budget, deadline, and contacts on one project | Website pages, events, and guest responses |
| **Communication** | Email from your Gmail or Outlook | Guest-facing pages, RSVPs, and guest messages |
| **Planning help** | Project **Chat** and **Revise with Envoy** | Guest tools and a lighter planning layer around the site |
| **Search before signup** | Yes — homepage contact search | Publishing a site starts with a Joy account |

## What Joy does well

Joy is strongest when the audience is **your guests**:

- A wedding website that is straightforward to publish and share
- RSVP tracking so you can see who has responded
- Guest list tools, including groups and events, without a heavy marketplace wrapped around them
- A calm, guest-first experience when you already know which professionals you plan to hire

Couples who want the website and the replies from guests in one place tend to start here. Joy can help you keep notes on professionals you have already chosen. The heavier work of searching a directory and emailing from your own inbox sits outside Joy.

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy focuses on **contacting and coordinating service providers** for the wedding as a project.

### Find contacts, then keep them

Search by wedding description and location on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you sign up, or during project creation from **Dashboard**. Add the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, and others as **Contacts** on one project. You decide who is on the list.

### A workspace for the service providers

**Overview** holds the wedding details contacts need: location, budget, and deadline. **Chat** helps you plan what to ask before you write. **Outreach** is where Envoy-drafted emails wait for your review. None of that is a public wedding website, and Envoy does not collect RSVPs.

### Email from you

Connect Gmail or Outlook under **Connected Email Accounts**. Envoy drafts the message; you use **Revise with Envoy** when you want a change, then approve the send. The thread is with that contact, and they reply to you.

### The same story for every contact

A guest site tells one public version of the wedding. Contacts often need a private, practical version — date, headcount, budget, and what you want from them. Envoy keeps that version on the project so you are not rewriting it from scratch for each email.

## When to use Joy

Choose **Joy** when:

- You need a wedding website guests will open
- RSVPs and the guest list are the task in front of you
- You already know your professionals and want a light place to track them beside the site
- The guest experience matters more than a project workspace for outreach

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- You still need to **find and contact** venues, florists, photographers, caterers, or other professionals
- Those people should share **one project** and one set of details
- You want drafts you can revise and **send from your own inbox**
- Replies and follow-up should stay in **Outreach** and in your email
- You want to search for contacts **before you create an account**

## Can you use both?

Yes. Publish the website and collect RSVPs in Joy. Run the search, the shortlist, and the emails in Envoy. Guests get a site. Contacts get a message from you, with the project behind it.

<Tip>
  Joy can hold the guest site. When you are ready to reach service providers, start with contact search on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com).
</Tip>
