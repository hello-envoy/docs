Keep **Notion AI** for writing, search, and edits inside the Notion pages and databases you already keep. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Notion. Most people keep both.

You can run the project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a wedding, a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding is a perfect fit.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, electrician, and the other businesses on the project, etc. They are not your personal address book, and they are not rows in a Notion database until you put them there yourself.

## Notion AI vs Envoy

Notion AI works inside Notion, on the docs and databases you already have. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Notion AI for** drafts, summaries, search, and edits in your Notion workspace.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Notion pages, databases, wikis, or meeting notes. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Notion AI is

Notion AI is Notion's assistant, built into the Notion workspace. It uses the pages, databases, and — when you turn them on — connected apps you already keep in Notion. You do not paste the whole wiki into a blank chat to get started.

### Writing and editing on the page

On a page, you can ask Notion AI to draft, rewrite, summarize, or translate, and you can highlight a passage and ask for an edit in place. The result lands in the doc your team already shares. That is the everyday use: the writing happens where the notes live.

### Notion Agent, search, and research

**Notion Agent** is the chat inside Notion. It can answer from your workspace, create or edit pages and databases, and take on a longer task across those pages. **Research Mode** pulls a deeper write-up when the question needs more than one page. Search can cover the workspace and, on plans that include connectors, apps such as Slack and Google Drive, so you can find an answer without opening every tab.

Eligible plans also include **AI Meeting Notes**, which transcribe a call and summarize it into Notion.

### Custom Agents

On Business and Enterprise plans, **Custom Agents** run repeatable work on a schedule or a trigger — a status update, a routed task, a reply in a connected tool — using the pages you grant them. They draw on Notion credits when they run. Other Notion AI features on those plans stay included. Agents act on the Notion content and connections you allow. They are not a search of Envoy's contact directory.

**Notion AI is for** the docs, databases, and meeting notes you already keep in Notion.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

Notion can stay the planning wiki. Envoy holds the shortlist, the shared details, and the email.

A planner can run the Envoy project for someone else and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that Envoy project. Sharing a Notion page with a partner is separate, and it stays in Notion.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project. A Notion database can track whatever columns you invent. Envoy's project is specifically the details, the Contacts, and the outreach.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. A Notion database of names is not Envoy's contact list, and Envoy does not import it.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**. A row in Notion is added on Envoy by you.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Ask what the caterer needs before they quote, or what to send the venue first. When planning is complete, Envoy prepares outreach drafts for the contacts on the project.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a fall wedding, about 140 guests. The planning wiki lives in Notion — budget table, question list, and a page per business you are considering. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the caterer include?" **Outreach:** Envoy drafts that note from the saved project details. Use **Draft with Envoy** if it is too long, then **Approve & Send**. The reply shows in your inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise** before you **Send reply**. The Notion page stays the place you and a partner edit notes together.

A fit-out uses the same split — the wiki in Notion, the electrician, painter, and the other contacts, etc., emailed from Envoy.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends.

**Draft with Envoy** rewrites an outreach draft. **Revise** is on the reply composer, after someone has written back.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Notion AI is built for **what is already in Notion** — and, with connectors, for apps you have linked. It can search those pages and draft from them. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up.

Use Notion as the wiki. Use Envoy to find Contacts and email them. For couple reviews, keep The Knot or WeddingWire open.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date. The venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc., need the same location, budget, and deadline.

In Notion, you can build a database, share it, and let Notion AI update pages or run a Custom Agent. Partners can edit that workspace together, under Notion's sharing. That collaboration is a real reason to keep Notion. It does not send from an Envoy inbox, and it does not add your partner to the Envoy project.

On Envoy, one coordinator runs the project and sends from the inbox they connect. **Overview** holds the details those emails use. **Chat** helps decide what each contact needs to hear.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Notion AI can **draft the words on a Notion page**. A Custom Agent can post to a tool you connected, on plans that include agents. That action follows Notion's permissions. It is not an Envoy **Outreach** thread, and Envoy does not import the page.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact you picked. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to **Outreach**. **Revise** rewrites the follow-up in the reply composer.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Notion AI availability depends on the Notion plan. Business and Enterprise include the main Notion AI features. Custom Agents on those plans use Notion credits. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical planning week:** the shared wiki in Notion, cited research in Perplexity if you want sources, couple reviews on The Knot, and the shortlist plus email on Envoy.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Notion AI for the pages and databases in Notion. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Notion AI's row is the workspace you already write in. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email.

| Category | Envoy | Notion AI | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Docs and databases in Notion | **Overview**, **Chat**, and **Outreach** for Contacts on this project | Writing, search, and edits inside the pages and databases already in your Notion workspace | **Notion AI** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed. They are not reviews | Can search and draft from pages you already stored. It does not return Envoy's contact list | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in | A draft can live on a Notion page. Sending to the business is a separate step | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, electrician, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | A database can track names you enter. It does not send Envoy outreach | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | You can keep notes in Notion before you open Envoy. Those notes are not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Draft with Envoy** | A question list or a rewrite on the Notion page | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A database row stays in Notion until you add that person on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise** rewrites a follow-up in the reply composer | The Notion page, unless you sent the note from your own mail | **Envoy** |

</Tip>

## Before you switch tools

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is search free after I sign up?">
    Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. Search stays available from **Dashboard**, project creation, and **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Envoy import my Notion pages?">
    No. Envoy does not import Notion pages, databases, meeting notes, or Custom Agent setup. Add Contacts from search, or with **+ New contact** and a name and email. Keep editing the wiki in Notion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my partner and I share a project?">
    One person runs the Envoy project: you, or a planner, friend, or family member coordinating for you. They send from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds your partner to that project. You can keep sharing the Notion workspace with your partner. That sharing stays in Notion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a contact never replies?">
    Envoy can't make them write back. The sent note stays on **Outreach**. Follow up on that thread, or email someone else. It is OK to email an **Unverified listing** after you check the address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I lose Notion if I start on Envoy?">
    No. Using Envoy does not replace Notion or Notion AI. Your pages and databases stay where they are. Most people keep both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone else run Envoy for us?">
    Yes. A planner, friend, or family member can create the Envoy project, add your Contacts, and send from the inbox they connect. They do not need access to your Notion workspace to do that, and Envoy will not grant it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Envoy badges reviews?">
    No. **Onboarded to Envoy** and **Unverified listing** tell you whether the business confirmed the listing. They are not star ratings. A Notion page of notes is not a review either.
  </Accordion>
