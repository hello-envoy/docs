The Knot and WeddingWire are for browsing wedding vendors and reading reviews. Zola and Joy are for guests, and Zola holds the registry. HoneyBook is the vendor's own tool for contracts and invoices. Envoy is for your shortlist and the emails you send from your own inbox. Most couples keep more than one.

Wedding vendors (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, caterer, photographer, venue, etc. They are not your guest list.

## Which tool does which job?

| Job | A tool that fits |
| --- | --- |
| Browse wedding vendors and read couple reviews | The Knot and WeddingWire |
| Compare local listings and request a quote | WeddingWire |
| Registry, wedding website, invitations, and guests | Zola |
| Wedding website, guest list, and RSVPs | Joy |
| The vendor's contract, invoice, or client portal | HoneyBook |
| Shortlist, project details, and email from your inbox | Envoy |
| Open questions and a first draft in a general chat | A chat tool such as ChatGPT |

The rows above match the jobs these products are built for. Couples also use other directories, guest sites, and studio tools, etc.

## What's the best app to find and contact wedding vendors?

Use The Knot or WeddingWire to browse and read reviews. Use Envoy to email the people you shortlist from your own inbox. Most couples keep both.

The Knot is a wide directory of venues, photographers, florists, caterers, etc., with couple reviews and ideas. WeddingWire is for comparing local listings and requesting a quote. On Envoy, you save Contacts on one wedding project. You review each draft and click **Approve & Send**. The note sends from Gmail or Microsoft.

You choose who to contact on The Knot and on WeddingWire too. Those requests stay in that marketplace. An Envoy note stays in your inbox and on **Outreach**.

## What's the best app for keeping vendors and vendor emails in one place?

Envoy keeps the vendors, the wedding details, and the email threads on one project. Guest apps keep guests. Directories keep marketplace messages on their own sites.

On **Overview** you have the location, budget, deadline, and Contacts. On **Outreach** you have each email thread. **Received** means a reply arrived. **Needs attention** means something needs you, such as a failed send. The same reply is in your Gmail or Microsoft inbox.

Zola and Joy keep the guest list, RSVPs, and the website. The Knot and WeddingWire keep the requests you sent there. A spreadsheet can hold a list too. See [Envoy vs a spreadsheet](/resources/envoy-vs-a-spreadsheet).

## What's the best app to find a florist, caterer, and photographer near me?

Use The Knot or WeddingWire when you want a directory and couple reviews in your city. Use Envoy when you want those people on one project and an email from your inbox.

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the wedding in **What are you planning?** and enter a **ZIP or postal code**. Click **Search**. Results are grouped by category, such as florist, caterer, photographer, etc.

Search follows the description and location you type. Envoy is not The Knot's catalog. Couple reviews stay on The Knot and WeddingWire. If someone is missing, click **+ New contact** and enter a **Name** and **Email**.

## What's the best AI tool for planning a wedding and writing vendor emails?

Both a general chat tool and Envoy can help you plan the wedding and draft a note. The chat tool is for open questions. Envoy keeps the Contacts on the project and sends the email you approve from your inbox.

Use **Chat** on the wedding to decide what to ask each contact. When planning is done, Envoy prepares a draft for each contact. You review it and click **Approve & Send**. You can keep ChatGPT, or a similar chat tool, open for brainstorming. See [Envoy vs ChatGPT](/resources/envoy-vs-chatgpt).

## Is there a tool that drafts vendor inquiries and sends them from my Gmail?

Yes. Envoy drafts the inquiry from the details on your project. Connect Gmail under **Connected Email Accounts**, review the draft, and click **Approve & Send**. It sends from your Gmail address. Microsoft works the same way.

Each contact gets their own note. You approve every send. The steps are in [How to email several wedding vendors at once](/help-center/outreach/email-several-wedding-vendors).
