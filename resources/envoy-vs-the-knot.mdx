The Knot is one of the largest wedding marketplaces in the U.S. Couples use it to browse venues, photographers, florists, and other wedding professionals, read reviews, and gather planning ideas. Envoy is built for the next job: **running the wedding as a project** and **emailing the contacts you choose**.

Use this page to see which tool fits each part of planning.

## At a glance

| | **Envoy** | **The Knot** |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Wedding directory, marketplace, and planning content |
| **Who it's for** | Anyone coordinating the wedding project | Couples discovering wedding professionals and ideas |
| **Who initiates contact** | You choose contacts and email them | You browse listings and request information in the marketplace |
| **Best for** | Venue, florist, photographer, caterer, and other contacts on one project | A large local directory, reviews, and wedding inspiration |
| **Project context** | Title, location, budget, deadline, and contacts together | Category listings plus planning content and tools |
| **Communication** | Email from your Gmail or Outlook | Requests and messages inside the marketplace |
| **Planning help** | Project **Chat** and **Revise with Envoy** | Articles, checklists, and directory tools |
| **Search before signup** | Yes — homepage contact search | You can browse the public directory |

## What The Knot does well

The Knot is a strong place to **see what is available** and learn the landscape of a city:

- A wide directory of wedding professionals, with reviews from couples
- Ideas, real weddings, and planning guidance under one brand
- Familiar category browsing — venues, photographers, florists, caterers, and more
- Listed businesses invest in advertising and leads so couples can find them

That is useful early, when you are still comparing styles, budgets, and who works in your area. The Knot Worldwide, The Knot's parent company, also offers wedding websites, registries, and invitations. Those guest-facing tools sit outside what Envoy does.

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy is the **coordination workspace** for the wedding itself. You keep the people, the details, and the emails on one project.

### You choose the contacts

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the wedding and search by location before you create an account. Review results, check badges such as **Onboarded to Envoy**, and select the people you want on the project. You can also search later from **Dashboard** while you create the project.

### One wedding, many contacts

A wedding spreads across categories. The venue, florist, photographer, and caterer all need the same date, location, guest count, and budget. Envoy keeps those **Contacts** on a single project — **Overview** for details, **Chat** for planning, and **Outreach** for email — so you are not starting a fresh inquiry for every listing.

### Outreach comes from your inbox

Envoy drafts the email. You read it, use **Revise with Envoy** when you want a tighter version, and send from your own Gmail or Outlook. Replies come back to you. You approve every message before it sends.

### Plan the questions before you write

**Chat** is scoped to this wedding. Use it to decide what to ask a venue versus a photographer, which details belong in the first email, and what to follow up on after someone replies.

## When to use The Knot

Choose **The Knot** when:

- You want a large directory of wedding professionals and couple reviews
- You are still browsing categories and collecting ideas
- A marketplace request is enough for a first look at who is nearby
- You also want a wedding website, registry, or guest tools from that ecosystem

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- You are coordinating **several contacts** for the same wedding
- You want to **decide who you email** and what the message says
- Date, location, budget, and deadline should stay on **one project**
- You want the thread in **your email**, where you will follow up
- You want to search for contacts **before you create an account**

## Can you use both?

Yes. Browse The Knot for ideas and reviews, then use Envoy to **shortlist contacts and run outreach** for the wedding as a whole. Envoy keeps the project and the emails. It does not host a wedding website, registry, or guest list.
