Keep **Gemini** for chat and for help inside the Google apps you already use. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Gemini. Most people keep both.

You can run the project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book.

## Gemini vs Envoy

Gemini is Google's assistant for questions, drafts, and help inside Google apps. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Gemini for** the Gemini app, and for drafting and answers inside Gmail, Docs, Drive, and the other Google apps your account includes.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Gemini's chat, image tools, or help inside Google Docs and Drive. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Gemini is

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. You can chat with the Gemini app on the web or on your phone. If your Google account includes it, Gemini also shows up inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, and Meet, so the help sits next to the file or the thread you already have open.

### The Gemini app

In the Gemini app you brainstorm, summarize a topic, draft an outline or an email, and ask follow-ups. You can turn on tools that look at the web, generate images, or run a deeper research pass that comes back with sources. The app is a general assistant with Google behind it.

### Gemini inside Google apps

When your account includes Gemini in the Google apps, you can ask for a draft in Gmail or Docs, get help in Sheets, and work from files in Drive without pasting everything into a blank chat. Workspace customers and personal Google AI plans differ in which of those features are included. The help still happens inside Google.

That Gmail draft is Gemini writing in Gmail. It is not an Envoy project, and it does not search Envoy for local service providers.

**Gemini is for** chat in the Gemini app and for help in the Google apps your account already includes.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

If you send mail from Gmail, Envoy can use that same mailbox — after you connect it and approve each send. Gemini can keep helping you inside Google. The shortlist and the project live in Envoy.

A planner can run the project for someone else and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. Gemini's answers are not Envoy's contact list.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**. Envoy does not import a Gemini chat or a Google Doc.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Ask what the florist needs in the first note, or what to send the photographer first. When planning is complete, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for the contacts on the project. You still review every send.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a Charleston weekend wedding, about 120 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date you need answers. You sketched the photographer note in a Google Doc with Gemini. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the photographer include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send** from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox you connected. You still review every send. The reply shows in that inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

A shop opening uses the same three tabs — signage, a cleaner, furniture delivery, etc.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends. Connecting Gmail lets Envoy send the notes you approve. It does not turn Envoy into Gemini, and it does not read your whole mailbox into the project.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Gemini is built for **answers and drafts**, including answers that can use Google Search and files in your Google account when that feature is available to you. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up.

Use Gemini to draft and to work in Google. Use Envoy to name the people on the project and email them.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date and one brief. On a wedding, that is the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc. A shop opening works the same way — signage, a cleaner, furniture delivery, etc.

In Google, you might keep a Doc, a Sheet, and a few Gmail threads, with Gemini helping in each one. Those files stay in Google. They are not an Envoy project.

On Envoy, those businesses are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. You update the project once, and the next draft can use those details.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Gemini in Gmail can **help you write** a message in Gmail, on accounts that include that feature. You still choose the recipient in Gmail. That thread is a Gmail thread. It is not filed on an Envoy project unless you sent it from Envoy.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact on the project. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to the **Outreach** tab. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

You can use both: Gemini for a Doc or a one-off Gmail note, Envoy when the note belongs to the shortlist.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Gemini features depend on the Google account you have. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical week:** draft the announcement in Docs with Gemini, keep guest details in the wedding tool you already use, and shortlist plus email the service providers on Envoy.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Gemini for chat and for help inside Google. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch. Connecting Gmail on Envoy does not replace Gemini in Gmail.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Gemini's row is help inside Google. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email.

| Category | Envoy | Gemini | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Help inside Google | Sends from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox you connect, to a Contact on the project | The Gemini app for chat, plus Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Drive, and other Google apps when your account includes it | **Gemini** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed. They are not reviews | Can suggest categories and draft questions. It does not return Envoy's contact list | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in | Gemini in Gmail can help write a message you address yourself | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | Docs, Sheets, and Gmail stay separate unless you assemble them | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | You can chat in Gemini before you open Envoy. That chat is not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Create draft**, the **Draft with Envoy** bar if you want a rewrite, and **Approve & Send** | A draft or an outline in Gemini or in a Google Doc | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A name in a Doc or chat stays in Google until you add it on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, before **Send reply** | The Gmail thread or the Gemini chat, depending on where you sent it | **Envoy** |
