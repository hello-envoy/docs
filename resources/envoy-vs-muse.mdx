Keep **Muse** for personal tasks you message it to handle. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

This page is about **Muse**, Meta's personal AI agent. You talk to it in the Muse app, on [muse.ai](https://muse.ai), or in WhatsApp. Meta introduced it in September 2026, and it is rolling out in the US on iOS and Android. It is not Adobe Muse, the discontinued website builder.

Using Envoy does not replace Muse. Most people keep both.

You can run an Envoy project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book, and they are not Muse's memory of your friends.

## Muse vs Envoy

Muse is a personal agent you message, the way you would message a person, and it can act across apps you connect. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Muse for** everyday personal tasks, goals, and follow-through in the Muse app or WhatsApp.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist of service providers.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Muse's personal agent, bookings, or the apps you connect to Muse. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Muse is

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. It is built to do tasks, not only to answer questions. You tell it what you need in the Muse app or in WhatsApp. It runs on its own computer in the cloud, with a browser, and it can keep working after you close the app. It comes back when something changes or when it needs a decision from you.

### Tasks, goals, and the apps you connect

People use Muse for personal follow-through: staying on top of a goal, turning that goal into a plan, and handling errands across services they connect. Meta's examples include sending an email, booking travel, and checking out on a purchase. You choose which apps Muse can use and how much it can do. For email, you choose whether it can read mail, send, or both. You can disconnect a service when you want.

Muse is built to check with you before sensitive steps, including sending an email or paying. It can also remember details you mentioned and bring them up later. You can tell it to forget something.

That is a personal agent for your life — calendar, errands, connected apps, and goals. It is not a directory of local service providers for a wedding or a remodel, and it does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

**Muse is for** personal tasks you want an agent to carry, in a conversation that feels like messaging someone.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

Muse can stay the agent for personal errands and goals. Envoy holds the shortlist of businesses, the shared project details, and the email you approve to those businesses.

A planner can run the Envoy project for someone else and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project. Messaging Muse in WhatsApp does not add your partner to Envoy.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project. **Chat** plans this project. It does not book personal travel or manage a grocery list.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. Muse's suggestions are not Envoy's contact list. Envoy does not import Muse conversations.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Ask what the florist needs in the first note, or what to send the venue first. When planning is complete, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for the contacts on the project. You still review every send.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a spring wedding, about 100 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date. You asked Muse, in WhatsApp, to hold the dinner reservation for a planning night and to remind you of the tasting. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the caterer include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send**. You still review every send. The reply shows in your inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

A remodel uses the same three tabs — a plumber, an electrician, a painter, etc. Muse can keep the personal reminders. The businesses on the job are Contacts in Envoy.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends.

Muse can also send email, if you gave it that access, and it is built to ask before it sends. That message is Muse acting in the mail account you connected there. It is not an Envoy project thread. Envoy does not import it, and connecting Gmail on Envoy does not connect Muse.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Muse is built for **personal tasks across apps you connect**. It can look things up and act in the browser on its computer. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show Envoy badges.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up, and search stays free after you sign in.

Use Muse for the personal agent. Use Envoy to name the service providers on the project and email them. For couple reviews of wedding businesses, keep The Knot or WeddingWire open.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date and one set of details. On a wedding, that is the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc. On a remodel, it is the plumber, electrician, painter, etc.

Muse can remember a goal and nudge the personal plan. It can work across the apps you connected. That memory is about you. It is not a shared project budget and deadline on a list of service-provider Contacts, and there is no Envoy **Overview** tab inside WhatsApp.

On Envoy, those businesses are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. You update the project once, and the next draft can use those details. One person sends from the inbox they connect.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Muse can **send email you approved** in the account you connected to Muse, and it can book or buy when you allow it. Use that for personal errands. Those messages stay in Muse's history and in that mailbox. They do not show up as Envoy **Outreach**.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact on the project. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to **Outreach**. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts on Envoy is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Muse has its own Meta account. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical week:** personal reminders and bookings in Muse, the planning wiki wherever you already keep it, couple reviews on The Knot if it is a wedding, and the shortlist plus email on Envoy.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Muse for personal tasks you message it to do. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch. Adobe Muse, the old website builder, is a different product and is not what this page compares.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Muse's row is the personal agent. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email to service providers.

| Category | Envoy | Muse | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| A personal agent for everyday tasks | The project, the Contacts, and email to those service providers | Message Muse in the Muse app or WhatsApp. It works on personal goals and connected apps, and checks with you before it sends email or pays | **Muse** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed. They are not reviews | Can look things up and act in apps you connect. It does not return Envoy's contact list or badges | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in. The thread is on **Outreach** | Can send mail you approved in an account you connected to Muse. That thread is not an Envoy project | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | Personal goals and connected apps. The businesses on a job are not an Envoy shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | You can message Muse before you open Envoy. That chat is not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Create draft**, the **Draft with Envoy** bar if you want a rewrite, and **Approve & Send** | A personal plan toward a goal you told Muse | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A detail Muse remembers stays in Muse until you add that business on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, before **Send reply** | The Muse conversation, and the mailbox Muse used if it sent the note | **Envoy** |

</Tip>
