Keep **Perplexity** when you want an answer with sources you can open. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Perplexity. Most people keep both.

You can run the project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book.

## Perplexity vs Envoy

Perplexity is where you ask a question and read an answer with sources. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Perplexity for** research answers with citations, follow-up questions, and source links you can check.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Perplexity's cited answers, or to attach a footnote to every sentence. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Perplexity is

Perplexity is an answer engine. You ask in plain language. It searches the web, reads pages, and answers with numbered sources you can open. Follow-up questions stay in the same thread, so the research can go one step deeper without starting over.

### Answers with sources

The core product is the cited answer. You might ask what a catering contract usually covers, which permits a storefront opening needs, or how far ahead photographers in a city tend to book. The reply points at the pages it used. You still judge whether those pages are right for your project. Citations are not customer reviews of a specific business on your shortlist.

### Deeper research

Perplexity can run a longer research pass that reads across many pages and comes back with a structured write-up, still with sources. That is useful when the question is broad — neighborhood patterns, typical timelines, questions to ask — and you want the trail of links.

### Computer, for longer workflows

On Perplexity Pro and Max, **Computer** is a separate worker for multi-step jobs: research, files, and actions across apps you connect, including mail. It is built for a deliverable that takes many steps. It is not a directory of local service providers with Envoy badges, and it does not create an Envoy project. Most people who compare Perplexity with Envoy are comparing the cited answer engine. Keep Computer in mind if you already use it for long jobs.

**Perplexity is for** questions you want answered with sources you can check.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

Perplexity can stay open for the research. Envoy holds the shortlist, the shared details, and the email.

A planner can run the project for someone else and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. A Perplexity answer with links is not Envoy's contact list, and the links are not **Onboarded to Envoy** badges.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review or a citation. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**. A business you found in a Perplexity source is added by you. Envoy does not import Perplexity threads.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Ask what the florist needs in the first note, given the date and guest count you saved. When planning is complete, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for the contacts on the project. You still review every send.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a Denver wedding next summer, about 90 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date you need answers. You used Perplexity to read how far ahead those businesses usually book, and you opened the sources. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the florist include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send**. You still review every send. The reply shows in your inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. Perplexity stays open when you want another cited answer.

A remodel uses the same three tabs — an electrician, a plumber, a painter, etc.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Perplexity is built for **research with sources**. Ask a planning question and read the links. It can name businesses that appear on the public web. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**. A citation is a page Perplexity used. It is not a customer review inside Envoy, and it is not a promise the business will reply.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up.

Use Perplexity to check the facts and the sources. Use Envoy to name the people on the project and email them. For couple reviews of wedding businesses, keep [The Knot](/resources/envoy-vs-the-knot) or WeddingWire open.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date and one city. The venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc., need the same location, budget, and deadline.

In Perplexity, each question is research. You can keep a thread of follow-ups. The sources stay in that thread. They are not contacts on a shared project.

On Envoy, those businesses are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. You update the project once, and the next draft can use those details. The research can stay in Perplexity while this coordination happens in Envoy.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Perplexity can **explain what to ask** and point at sources. You still send the email yourself, unless you are using a connected-app workflow such as Computer on a plan that includes it. Even then, that send is Perplexity's workflow. It is not an Envoy **Outreach** thread, and Envoy does not import it.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact you picked. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to **Outreach**. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Perplexity has its own account. Some research tools and Computer depend on the Perplexity plan you have. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical planning week:** cited research in Perplexity, couple reviews on The Knot or WeddingWire, guest list on Zola or Joy, and the shortlist plus email on Envoy.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Perplexity for answers with sources. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Perplexity's row is the cited answer. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email.

| Category | Envoy | Perplexity | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web research with sources | Search returns Contacts for the project you describe. Badges are not citations or reviews | An answer with sources you can open, plus follow-up questions | **Perplexity** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed | Can mention businesses that appear in sources. It does not return Envoy's contact list | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in | You leave with sources and a draft of what to ask, then send from your own mail | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | Each thread is research. The shared brief is whatever you ask next | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | You can research before you open Envoy. Those links are not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Create draft**, the **Draft with Envoy** bar if you want a rewrite, and **Approve & Send** | A sourced list of questions to consider | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A name in a source stays in the thread until you add it on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, before **Send reply** | The Perplexity thread, unless you sent the note from your own mail | **Envoy** |

    Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. Search stays available from **Dashboard**, project creation, and **Contacts**.

    No. Envoy does not import Perplexity answers, sources, or Computer workflows. Add the people you want to email as Contacts — from search, or with **+ New contact** and a name and email.

    One person runs the Envoy project: you, or a planner, friend, or family member coordinating for you. They send from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds your partner to that project. Your partner can keep using Perplexity.
