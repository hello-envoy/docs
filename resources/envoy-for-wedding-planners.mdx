A wedding planner can email vendors for a client's wedding from the planner's own Gmail or Microsoft inbox. You run that project in Envoy. You are the one sender. Your contracts and invoices stay in the studio tools you already use, such as HoneyBook.

Wedding vendors (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, caterer, photographer, venue, etc. They are not the couple's guest list, and they are not the client list in your studio software.

A friend or family member can run this same kind of project and send from the inbox they connect.

## What's the best tool for a wedding planner to email vendors for a client?

Use Envoy when you are finding vendors and emailing them for one client's wedding. Keep HoneyBook, or the other studio tool you already use, for proposals, contracts, and invoices. You can keep both.

Envoy holds the Contacts, the location, the budget, the deadline, and the email you approve. HoneyBook holds the studio's clients and the documents you send them. The couple can keep The Knot, WeddingWire, Zola, or Joy for browsing, guests, and the registry.

## How can a planner send vendor inquiries from their own email?

Create the client's wedding as a project in your Envoy account, add the Contacts, and connect your inbox. Envoy drafts a separate note for each contact. You click **Approve & Send**. The message comes from your address.

One person runs the project. There is no invite that adds the couple to that project.

1. Sign in to your own Envoy account.
2. From **Dashboard**, click **+ New project**.
3. On **Essentials**, enter a **Title**, a **Description**, and a **Location** for the couple's wedding.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. On **Contacts**, select the vendors to add, such as a venue, florist, caterer, etc.
6. Click **Create project**. The project opens on **Overview**.
7. On **Overview**, click **Edit**.
8. Add the **Budget** and **Deadline**, then click **Save**.
9. Optional: Click **+ New contact** and enter a **Name** and **Email** for a vendor you already know.
10. Open **Account** and go to **Connected Email Accounts**.
11. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**, then finish signing in. Outreach sends from the inbox marked **Primary**.
12. Open **Chat** and decide what each vendor should hear. When you are ready, Envoy prepares a draft for each contact.
13. Open **Outreach**, read the first draft, and click **Approve & Send**.
14. Repeat for the next contact. Each vendor gets their own note.

The reply arrives in your inbox and on that contact's thread. **Received** means they replied.

## Is this the same as a Pro account?

Emailing vendors for a client uses a consumer project. A **Pro** account is for a service provider who wants to receive inquiries through Envoy. A coordinator account does not receive inquiries for services you sell.

Register as a **Pro** when you want couples to find your planning business on Envoy. See [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro). You can still use a consumer project to email vendors for a client.

## Where do the client's contracts and invoices stay?

They stay in your studio tools. HoneyBook holds proposals, contracts, invoices, and a client portal. Envoy keeps the vendor list and the email you approve for this wedding.

If a photographer already sent a HoneyBook link, the couple can keep using that link. Your Envoy note to the florist is separate. You don't have to switch.
