If you plan weddings for several clients a year, you can email each client's vendors from your own Gmail or Microsoft inbox. Each client wedding is its own project on **Dashboard**. You are the one sender. Your contracts and invoices stay in the studio tools you already use, such as HoneyBook.

Wedding vendors (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, caterer, photographer, venue, etc. They are not the couple's guest list, and they are not the client list in your studio software.

## What's the best tool for a wedding planner to email vendors for a client?

Use Envoy when you are finding vendors and emailing them for a client's wedding. Keep HoneyBook, or the other studio tool you already use, for proposals, contracts, and invoices. You can keep both.

Envoy holds the Contacts, the location, the budget, and the email you approve for each wedding. HoneyBook holds the studio's clients and the documents you send them. The couple can keep The Knot, WeddingWire, Zola, or Joy for browsing, guests, and the registry.

Your preferred vendors stay in **Contacts**. On a new client's project, click **+ Attach existing** to add a florist or photographer you already saved for another wedding.

## How can a planner send vendor inquiries from their own email?

Create the client's wedding as a project in your Envoy account, add the Contacts, and connect your inbox. Envoy drafts a separate note for each contact. You click **Send**. The message comes from your address.

1. Sign in to your own Envoy account.
2. From **Dashboard**, click **+ New project**.
3. On **Essentials**, enter a **Title**, a **Description**, and a **Location** for the couple's wedding. Put the wedding date and guest count in the **Description**.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. On **Contacts**, select the vendors to add, such as a venue, florist, caterer, etc.
6. Click **Create project**. The project opens on **Overview**.
7. Optional: On **Overview**, click **Edit**. Add the **Budget** and a **Deadline** (the date you need answers by), then click **Save**.
8. Optional: Click **+ Attach existing** to add vendors you already saved, or click **+ New contact** and enter a **Name** and **Email**.
9. Open **Account** and go to **Connected Email Accounts**.
10. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**, then finish signing in. Outreach sends from the inbox marked **Primary** (the inbox your emails come from).
11. Open **Chat** and decide what each vendor should hear. When you are ready, Envoy prepares a draft for each contact.
12. Open **Outreach** and read the first draft. Make sure the date and guest count are in the note before you approve it.
13. Click **Send**.
14. Repeat for the next contact. Each vendor gets a personal note, not a blast. That's why you approve them one at a time.

The reply arrives in your inbox and on that contact's thread. **Received** means they replied.

One person runs the project. The couple won't see the Envoy project. Share updates the way you already do.

## Will vendors know an app sent it?

The note comes from your own email address and is signed with your name. You read and approve every note before it goes.

## Which inbox do the notes come from?

Notes come from the inbox marked **Primary**. To send from a different inbox, open **Account** and go to **Connected Email Accounts**. Click **Replace with Gmail** or **Replace with Microsoft** and sign in to the other inbox. The new inbox becomes **Primary**, and the old one is disconnected.

## What does Envoy do with my inbox?

Envoy sends the notes you approve and brings vendor replies into **Outreach**. You can disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**. See [Connect your email](/help-center/outreach/connect-your-email).

## Is this the same as a Pro account?

No. Your consumer account is for emailing vendors on a client's behalf. A **Pro** account is how couples find and contact your planning business on Envoy.

Each Envoy account is one type. To have both, register the **Pro** account with a different Google or Microsoft account. See [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro).

## Where do the client's contracts and invoices stay?

They stay in your studio tools. HoneyBook holds proposals, contracts, invoices, and a client portal. Envoy keeps the vendor list and the email you approve for this wedding.

Vendor contracts and invoices still come to you or the couple the way they do today. If a photographer already sent a HoneyBook link, the couple can keep using that link. Your Envoy note to the florist is separate. You don't have to switch.
