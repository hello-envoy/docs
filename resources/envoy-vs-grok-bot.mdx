Keep **Grok Bot** for work you hand to an always-on Bot. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Grok Bot. They answer different jobs. Grok Bot is Cursor's Grok Bot, an xAI Grok-style desktop assistant for chat and tasks. It is not Envoy.

You can run an Envoy project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book.

## Grok Bot vs Envoy

Grok Bot is where you message a Bot and let it carry a multi-step task on its own computer. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people who use both keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Grok Bot for** named Bots that work across apps and sites on a cloud computer, including after you close the laptop.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Grok Bot's computer, routines, or coding and operations tasks. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Grok Bot is

Grok Bot is Cursor's product for AI teammates you message, in the style of xAI's Grok assistant. You create a Bot, describe the job, and grant access as it asks. The same Bot is available in the Grok Bot desktop app (macOS, Windows, and Linux) and on iPhone.

This page is about that product. It is not the Envoy help center, and a Bot is not an Envoy project.

### Bots on their own computer

Each Bot works on a persistent cloud computer with a browser, files, and a terminal. Your Bots on one account share that computer, including files and signed-in browser sessions. Work continues while your laptop is closed. The Bot uses a connector when one exists, and the browser when it does not. It comes back in the conversation when something needs your approval.

You message a Bot the way you would message a teammate. Setup is the message, not a workflow you have to build first. A Bot can keep memory, files, and preferences across sessions. You can walk it through a task once and save that path to run again.

### What people hand a Bot

People use Grok Bot for work that spans tools and has a finish line: research, documents, operations, bug fixes, and routines that should keep going. A Bot can draft in the apps you let it use. A draft a Bot leaves in some other inbox is that Bot's work. It is not an Envoy **Outreach** thread, and Envoy does not import it.

Grok Bot access is included with paid individual Cursor plans and the Cursor Teams plan. You can also link a SuperGrok subscription. That access is separate from Envoy. This page does not compare subscription prices.

**Grok Bot is for** handing a multi-step task to a Bot that works on its own computer and checks back when it needs you.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

Grok Bot can stay the place you delegate a computer task. Envoy holds the shortlist, the shared details, and the email you approve.

A planner can run the Envoy project for someone else and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project. Messaging a Grok Bot from your phone is a different login.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. A Bot browsing the web is not Envoy's contact list, and it does not apply **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**. A name a Bot found is added on Envoy by you.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. It plans what to ask the people on the project. It does not drive a separate cloud computer, and it does not keep working as a Bot after you close the laptop.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a Charleston weekend wedding, about 120 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date you need answers. You asked a Grok Bot to pull a checklist of what to ask the venue and leave it for you to review. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the photographer include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send**. You still review every send. The reply shows in your inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

A home addition uses the same three tabs — a framer, an electrician, a roofer, etc. A Bot can research in the background. The shortlist and the approved email stay on the Envoy project.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends. Every outreach send waits for that approval. Envoy does not hand the mailbox to a Bot.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Grok Bot is built to **go do a task** on its computer: open sites, read files, and come back with a result you review. It can look businesses up on the web when you ask. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show Envoy badges.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up, and search stays free after you sign in.

Use a Bot for the open-ended computer task. Use Envoy to name the people on the project and email them.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date and one brief. On a wedding, that is the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc. A home addition works the same way — a framer, an electrician, a roofer, etc.

A Grok Bot can research, draft, and remind you, and several Bots can work at once on the shared computer. Their context is the Bot's memory and the files on that computer. It is not an Envoy project with a shared budget and deadline on every Contact.

On Envoy, those businesses are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. You update the project once, and the next draft can use those details.

## How outreach and follow-up work

A Grok Bot can **draft a message** in an app you have signed in on its computer, and it is built to pause for approval on steps that need you. That draft lives in the Bot's work. Envoy does not import it, and it does not appear on **Outreach** unless you sent it from the Envoy project.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact you picked. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to **Outreach**. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts on Envoy is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Grok Bot has its own access through Cursor or SuperGrok. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical week:** hand a research or file task to a Grok Bot, keep couple reviews on The Knot if it is a wedding, and shortlist plus email the service providers on Envoy.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Grok Bot for tasks you want a Bot to finish on its own computer. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Grok Bot's row is the always-on task. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email.

| Category | Envoy | Grok Bot | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand off a task that keeps running | You approve each outreach email on the project. Search and send stay on that project | Named Bots work on a cloud computer across apps and sites, including after you close the laptop, and come back when they need approval | **Grok Bot** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed. They are not reviews | Can browse and summarize what it finds. It does not return Envoy's contact list or badges | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in. The thread is on **Outreach** | A Bot can draft in an app you signed it into. That draft is not an Envoy project | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | Bots share a computer and can message each other. The brief is the task you gave them | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | A Bot can research before you open Envoy. That research is not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Create draft**, the **Draft with Envoy** bar if you want a rewrite, and **Approve & Send** | A checklist or a draft the Bot leaves for you to review | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A name in the Bot's notes stays there until you add it on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, before **Send reply** | The Bot conversation, and whatever app the Bot was using | **Envoy** |

  Keep Grok Bot for the task you want finished in the background. When you want to email a shortlist of Contacts, try the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) — search is free before you sign up. You don't have to switch.
</Tip>

## Before you switch tools

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Grok Bot the same product as Envoy?">
    No. Grok Bot is Cursor's assistant for chat and tasks. You message Bots that work on a cloud computer in the Grok Bot app. Envoy is a project workspace for service-provider Contacts and email from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox you connect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is search free after I sign up?">
    Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. Search stays available from **Dashboard**, project creation, and **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will Envoy import a Grok Bot's drafts?">
    No. Envoy does not import Bot conversations, files, or messages a Bot drafted in another app. Add Contacts from search, or with **+ New contact** and a name and email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my partner and I share a project?">
    One person runs the Envoy project: you, or a planner, friend, or family member coordinating for you. They send from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds your partner to that project. A Grok Bot on your Cursor account stays on that account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a contact never replies?">
    Envoy can't make them write back. The sent note stays on **Outreach**. Follow up on that thread, or email someone else. It is OK to email an **Unverified listing** after you check the address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I lose Grok Bot if I start on Envoy?">
    No. Using Envoy does not replace Grok Bot. Your Bots stay in the Grok Bot app. Most people who want both keep both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone else run Envoy for us?">
    Yes. A planner, friend, or family member can create the Envoy project, add your Contacts, and send from the inbox they connect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
