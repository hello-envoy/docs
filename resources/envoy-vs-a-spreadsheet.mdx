Both a spreadsheet and Envoy can hold a list of wedding vendors. A spreadsheet is flexible, and it is easy to share with a partner or a client. Envoy also keeps each email thread with that vendor. You can see who replied and what needs your attention.

Wedding vendors (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, caterer, photographer, venue, etc. They are not your guest list.

## What can I use instead of a spreadsheet to track wedding vendors?

You can keep the spreadsheet, use Envoy, or use both. The spreadsheet holds whatever columns you set up. Envoy holds the same people as Contacts on one wedding project, next to the email you send them.

|  | Spreadsheet | Envoy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A list of vendors | Any columns you set up, such as name, email, type, etc. | **Contacts** on the project, with **Name**, **Email**, and an optional **Type** |
| Share the list | Share the file with a partner or a client | One person runs the project and sends from the inbox they connect |
| Wedding details in one place | Columns you add for city, budget, date, etc. | **Location**, **Budget**, and **Deadline** in **Project Details** |
| A note that you emailed someone | A column you update | Status **Sent** in **Inbox** |
| Who replied | A column you update | Status **Received** in **Inbox** |
| The email thread | Your inbox, or text you paste into a cell | The thread in **Inbox**, and your inbox |
| The first email | You write it and send it yourself | Envoy drafts it. You click **Send** |
| Booked status, deposits, and payment dates | Columns you add | Booked status is not tracked. Envoy tracks the emails, not the contract. Coming soon: a **Budget** view with a **Deposits and payments** list, so you can record deposits after you pay elsewhere. Envoy doesn't take payments |

## How do I track which wedding vendors I contacted and who replied?

Open the wedding, click **Contacts**, then **Inbox**. Each contact is listed there. Expand a contact to read the thread.

**Sent** means the note went out from your inbox. **Received** means a reply arrived from that contact. **Needs attention** means something needs your action, such as a failed send. Use **Received** to focus on replies. Use **Needs attention** to focus on threads that need you.

The same messages are in the Gmail or Microsoft inbox you connected. **Inbox** lists those threads for this wedding.

## When is a spreadsheet enough?

Keep the spreadsheet when you want a file that is easy to share, or columns that match a checklist you already use. You can sort it and send the file to a partner or a client.

Keep the spreadsheet for booked status and the contract. Envoy tracks the emails, not the contract. A **Budget** view with a **Deposits and payments** list is [coming soon](#coming-soon-to-envoy). Envoy doesn't take payments. A spreadsheet can still be easier to share with a partner.

On Envoy, one person runs the project and sends from their own inbox. Your partner can keep the spreadsheet, The Knot, Zola, or Joy.

## When does Envoy carry the email too?

Use Envoy when you want the list and the email on the same project. **Ask Envoy** helps you decide what to ask. Envoy drafts a separate note for each contact from the details you saved. You review it and click **Send**. The reply stays on that contact's thread.

A spreadsheet can record that you wrote to someone. You still write, send, and file the reply yourself. You can keep a spreadsheet for a plan you share, and use Envoy for the inbox. You don't have to switch.

## Coming soon to Envoy

These Envoy features are on the way. They aren't in the app yet, and the page will change when they arrive.

Many people add columns for tasks, deposits, and side-by-side notes. These bring that onto the Envoy project, next to each email thread.

- **Timeline and tasks:** on **Project**, a planning **Timeline** shows milestones by month (for example, book the venue or lock catering) and opens each one into its tasks. You can add a task and give it an owner and a due date. This is a planning calendar of tasks, not a wedding-day run-of-show or seating chart.
- **Due this week on Today:** **Today** lists the tasks **Due this week** and the emails that **Need attention**, so you can see the wedding's next steps when you open it.
- **Budget:** on **Project**, a **Budget** view shows what's budgeted, booked, spent, and remaining, with a chart of where the money goes. A **Deposits and payments** list keeps each deposit in date order, and you can mark a deposit paid after you pay it elsewhere. Envoy doesn't take payments.
- **Compare:** under **Contacts**, pick a type of service provider, such as florists, and check two to four of them to see them side by side. Comparing never sends an email.
- **More on Dashboard:** for planners with several weddings, **Dashboard** shows a **Needs you** list across all of them, days to go for each wedding, and a **Next 12 months** view.
