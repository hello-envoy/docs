Keep **ChatGPT** for open-ended chat, explanations, and writing. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace ChatGPT. Most people keep both.

You can run the project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book.

## ChatGPT vs Envoy

ChatGPT is where you ask, brainstorm, and draft in an open conversation. You can also brainstorm in Envoy **Chat**, on the details saved for this project. Envoy keeps a shortlist of Contacts and sends email from Gmail or Microsoft after you approve it. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Both can** suggest service providers, brainstorm the wedding, and draft a note. ChatGPT does that in an open chat, including the web when search is on. Envoy does it as Contacts on one project.
- **Keep ChatGPT for** open-ended chat, explanations, and rewriting that are not tied to this project.
- **Use Envoy for** durable Contacts, **Overview** / **Chat** / **Outreach**, and email you approve from Gmail or Microsoft.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace ChatGPT's general chat, image generation, or coding help. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What ChatGPT is

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational assistant. You type a question or paste a draft, and it answers in that thread. People use it to think something through, write a first version, and ask the next question without leaving the chat.

### Chat, writing, and explanations

The conversation is the product. You can ask ChatGPT to explain a topic, brainstorm options, draft a note, or rewrite something you already wrote. It follows the turns in that chat, so you can steer the next answer. That open-ended back-and-forth is what people open ChatGPT for.

### Search, files, and images

ChatGPT can look up current information on the web when you want an answer tied to sources, and it can read files you upload — a PDF, a spreadsheet, a photo — and talk about what is in them. It can also create or edit images from a description. Those tools sit inside the same chat.

### Longer jobs, still in ChatGPT

On accounts that include them, ChatGPT can take on a longer piece of work: research across sources, a document or file you review, or software work in Codex in the desktop app. Those jobs still live in ChatGPT. They use your ChatGPT account and the tools that account includes. They are a different product from a directory of service providers and a project inbox.

**ChatGPT is for** questions, everyday writing, and ideas in a chat you steer.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

ChatGPT can stay open for the general questions. Envoy holds the shortlist, the shared details, and the email.

A planner can run the project for someone else: add the Contacts, keep the location, budget, and deadline on the project, and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project. You are not retyping the brief into a fresh chat for each business.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search you do before sign-in, so you can try Envoy without an account. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project. It is not a forever ceiling.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result in your city means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. Envoy is not a review site, and it does not pull in ChatGPT's answers.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge tells you when they will reply, and neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

People you already have do not have to come from search. On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On the project **Overview** tab, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** to put someone from your directory on the project. A name you found in a ChatGPT chat is added by you. Envoy does not import ChatGPT threads.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Envoy **Chat** only knows this project's saved details, not a general chat history. Ask what to send a venue first, what a florist needs to quote, or what to prioritize if the date is close. When planning is complete, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for the contacts on the project. You still review every send.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a Charleston weekend wedding, about 120 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date you need answers. You used ChatGPT the night before to list what a first note should cover. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the photographer include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send**. You still review every send. The reply shows in your inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. ChatGPT stays open for the next general question.

A kitchen remodel uses the same three tabs — cabinets, counters, an electrician, etc.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project — you, or someone coordinating for you — who wants one workspace for the service providers and email they control.

## How you find service providers

Both can help you find service providers. They do it differently.

ChatGPT suggests categories and names in the conversation. With search on, it can look businesses up on the web and talk through what it found. You still check the address. Those names are not Envoy Contacts, and ChatGPT does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

Envoy search on the [homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) uses your description and ZIP or postal code, before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before sign-in) and attach the people you pick to the project. Badges say whether the business confirmed the listing. They are not reviews. If someone is missing, add them with **+ New contact**.

Use ChatGPT for ideas and a conversation. Use Envoy when you want those people saved as Contacts on the wedding.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Both can hold one brief for several businesses. A wedding is the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc., sharing one date. A remodel works the same way — an electrician, a plumber, a cabinet maker, etc.

In ChatGPT, that context lives in the chat you keep typing into. A new chat does not inherit the Envoy project. You can paste details in, and you can ask for a checklist. The conversation stays in ChatGPT.

On Envoy, those people are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. You update the project once, and the next draft can use those details. The couple can brainstorm together in ChatGPT, and they can also brainstorm in Envoy **Chat** on this project's saved details. Only Envoy outreach sends from the one connected inbox.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Both can draft the note. ChatGPT writes it in the chat. You copy it into mail, or you use it while you edit the Envoy draft. ChatGPT does not file that reply on **Outreach**.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact you picked, including one you added by name. Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. Rewrite it with the **Draft with Envoy** bar (**Go** / **Undo**) if you want. **Approve & Send** goes from Gmail or Microsoft. You still review every send. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. The thread is in your inbox and on **Outreach**. One person sends. There is no shared login.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

ChatGPT has its own account. This page is about which job each product fits. It does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical week:** chat and drafts in ChatGPT, cited research in Perplexity, couple reviews on The Knot or WeddingWire, and the shortlist plus email on Envoy. Contracts and invoices stay with each business and their own tools.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep ChatGPT for open-ended chat and everyday writing. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch.
