Keep **Microsoft Copilot** for chat and for help inside the Microsoft 365 apps you already use. Use **Envoy** when you need **Contacts**, a project workspace, and email from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Copilot. Most people keep both.

You can run the project yourself, or a planner, friend, or family member can coordinate it and send from the inbox they connect. The same workspace fits a home remodel, a small-business fit-out, and other projects with more than one service provider. A wedding fits the same pattern: several vendors, one date, one brief.

Service providers (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. They are not your personal address book.

## Microsoft Copilot vs Envoy

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's assistant for questions, drafts, and work inside Microsoft 365. Envoy is where you keep a shortlist of Contacts on one project and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. Most people keep both.

## At a glance

- **Keep Microsoft Copilot for** web-grounded chat, and for drafting and answers in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams when your account includes them.
- **Use Envoy for** Contacts, one project, and email from your inbox once you have a shortlist.
- **Don't expect Envoy to** replace Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or Teams, or Copilot's answers over your work files. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, contracts, and a day-of plan stay in the products you already use for those jobs.

Details are below. The [comparison matrix](#comparison-matrix) is at the end.

## What Microsoft Copilot is

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant. People meet it in the Copilot app on the web, on mobile, and on the desktop, and inside Microsoft 365. How much of your work it can see depends on the Microsoft subscription you have.

### Copilot Chat

Copilot Chat is the conversation: ask a question, summarize a topic, rewrite a paragraph, analyze a file you upload, or create an image. For eligible Microsoft 365 users, that chat is grounded in the web and covered by your organization's data protections. It is a general assistant next to your Microsoft account.

Copilot Pages, on plans that include them, let you pull a chat result onto a page you can edit and share. Agents in Copilot can take on repeatable tasks. Those surfaces stay in Microsoft's apps.

### Copilot inside Microsoft 365

With **Microsoft 365 Copilot**, Copilot also works in the apps: draft and rewrite in Word, build and explain in Excel, start a deck in PowerPoint, help in Outlook, and catch up in Teams. On that license, answers can draw on your work mail, files, meetings, and calendar through Microsoft's work data, not only on the public web. Organizations without that license still have the lighter Copilot Chat experience. The in-app help is the reason many people compare Copilot with a general chatbot.

Copilot in Outlook can help you write a message in Outlook. That is Microsoft helping inside your mailbox. It is not a search of Envoy's contact directory, and it does not create an Envoy project.

**Microsoft Copilot is for** chat and for getting work done inside Microsoft 365, with deeper use of your work files when you have Microsoft 365 Copilot.

## What Envoy is

Envoy is for **running the work as a project** and contacting the service providers on it. In the product, those service providers are **Contacts**.

If you send mail from Microsoft, Envoy can use that mailbox — after you connect it under **Connected Email Accounts** and approve each send. Copilot can keep helping you in Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams. The shortlist and the project live in Envoy.

A planner can run the project for someone else and send from the inbox they connect. There is no shared login and no invite that adds a second person to that project. A shared Copilot page or a Teams channel is a different product.

### Project workspace

Each effort is a project with three tabs:

| Tab | What you use it for |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Overview** | Title, description, location, budget, deadline, and the contacts on this project |
| **Chat** | Plan with Envoy — scope, priorities, and what to ask before you write |
| **Outreach** | Review drafts, revise them, send, and follow up when someone replies |

The same location, budget, and deadline stay attached to every contact on that project.

### Contacts and discovery

On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe the project and enter a ZIP or postal code before you create an account. Results are grouped by category. You can start a shortlist (up to 8 before you create an account). That limit applies only to the search before sign-in. After you sign in, add more from **Dashboard**, the **Contacts** page, or the project.

Search follows the description and location you type. A thin result means try a more specific description, or add the business you already know. Copilot can talk about a file you already have. It does not return Envoy's contact results.

**Onboarded to Envoy** means the business confirmed this listing. **Unverified listing** means someone else added it and the business has not confirmed it. It is OK to email an unverified listing — check the address yourself first. Neither badge is a review. See [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained).

On **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** and enter a name and email. On **Overview**, use **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing**. Envoy does not import a Word document, an Outlook thread, or a Copilot chat.

### Chat

**Chat** is scoped to this project. It uses the title, description, location, budget, and deadline you saved. Ask what the venue needs in the first note, or what to prioritize if the date is close. When planning is complete, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for the contacts on the project. You still review every send.

### A pass through one project

**Overview:** a Charleston weekend wedding, about 120 guests. Venue and photographer first, then florist, caterer, etc., with the budget and the date you need answers. You outlined the scope in Word with Copilot. **Chat:** "What should the first note to the photographer include?" **Outreach:** Click **Create draft**. Envoy writes a first pass from the project details. To rewrite it, use the **Draft with Envoy** bar, enter instructions, and click **Go**. **Undo** restores the previous wording. Then click **Approve & Send** from the Microsoft or Gmail inbox you connected. You still review every send. The reply shows in that inbox and on **Outreach**. In the reply composer, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

An office fit-out uses the same three tabs — electrical, painting, furniture install, etc.

### Outreach from your inbox

Envoy only uses email related to the contacts you selected. It can prepare drafts, send messages you approve, and show those threads in **Outreach**. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.

Connect Gmail or Microsoft under **Connected Email Accounts**. The message comes from your address. You approve it before it sends. Connecting Microsoft lets Envoy send the notes you approve. It does not turn on Copilot in Word or Teams, and it does not grant Copilot access to the Envoy project.

Click **Create draft** for the first pass. Envoy writes it from the project details. The two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar rewrites that open draft: enter instructions and click **Go**, or click **Undo** to restore the previous wording. On a reply, click **Revise**, review the preview, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**. You still review every send.

**Envoy is for** the person running the project who wants the service providers and the email in one place.

## How you find service providers

Copilot is built for **answers and drafts**, including drafts inside Microsoft 365 and, with Microsoft 365 Copilot, answers that can use your work mail and files. It does not return Envoy's contact results for your ZIP code, and it does not show **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.

Envoy is built for a **shortlist tied to a project**, including people you type in yourself. Homepage search works before you sign up.

Use Copilot to work in Microsoft 365. Use Envoy to name the people on the project and email them.

## How you coordinate more than one service provider

Several businesses share one date and one brief. On a wedding, that is the venue, florist, photographer, caterer, etc. An office fit-out works the same way — electrical, painting, furniture install, etc.

In Microsoft 365, you might keep a Word brief, an Excel budget, and Outlook threads, with Copilot helping in each app. Those files follow your Microsoft permissions. They are not an Envoy project.

On Envoy, those businesses are contacts on **one project**. **Overview** holds the details. **Chat** helps you decide what each contact needs to hear. You update the project once, and the next draft can use those details.

## How outreach and follow-up work

Copilot in Outlook can **help you write** inside Outlook, on accounts that include it. You choose the recipient there. That thread stays in Outlook. It shows on Envoy **Outreach** when you sent it from the Envoy project.

Envoy outreach is **your email** to a contact on the project. You approve the draft and send it from Gmail or Microsoft. Replies come back to that inbox and to **Outreach**. On a reply, click **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, and **Send reply**.

You can use both: Copilot for the Word scope and for day-to-day Outlook, Envoy when the note belongs to the shortlist of service providers.

## What stays free to search

Searching for contacts is free before and after you sign up. You can keep searching from **Dashboard**, while you create a project, and from **Contacts**.

Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot depend on the Microsoft plan you have. This page does not compare subscription prices.

<Note>
  **A typical week:** write the scope in Word with Copilot, keep the guest list in the wedding tool you already use, and shortlist plus email the service providers on Envoy. Contracts stay with each business.
</Note>

## Conclusion

Keep Microsoft Copilot for help inside Microsoft 365. Use Envoy when you're ready to put service providers on a project and email them from your inbox. You don't have to switch. Connecting Microsoft on Envoy does not replace Copilot in Word, Excel, Outlook, or Teams.

### Comparison matrix

This isn't a winner-take-all score — each product wins the job it's built for. Copilot's row is Microsoft 365. Envoy's rows are the contacts, the project, and the email.

| Category | Envoy | Microsoft Copilot | Best for… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Help inside Microsoft 365 | Sends from the Microsoft or Gmail inbox you connect, to a Contact on the project | Chat on the web, plus drafting in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams. With Microsoft 365 Copilot, answers can use your work mail, files, and meetings | **Microsoft Copilot** |
| Find service providers for this project | Homepage search by description and location. Badges show whether a listing is confirmed. They are not reviews | Can draft questions from a file you already have. It does not return Envoy's contact list | **Envoy** |
| Email a shortlist from your inbox | You approve a Gmail or Microsoft note to a contact you searched or typed in | Copilot in Outlook can help write a message you address yourself | **Envoy** |
| One project, many contacts | Florist, photographer, caterer, venue, etc. share location, budget, and deadline | Word, Excel, and Outlook stay in Microsoft 365 unless you assemble them | **Envoy** |
| Start a shortlist before an account | Up to 8 contacts before you create an account. Add more after you sign in | You can chat with Copilot before you open Envoy. That chat is not the shortlist | **Envoy** |
| Plan what this project should ask | **Chat** on your saved details, then **Create draft**, the **Draft with Envoy** bar if you want a rewrite, and **Approve & Send** | A brief in Word or a prompt in Copilot Chat | **Envoy** |
| People you already know | **+ New contact** or **+ Attach existing** with a name and email | A name in a document stays in Microsoft 365 until you add it on the project | **Envoy** |
| Where a reply shows up | Your inbox and the project's **Outreach** thread. **Revise**, then **Apply revision** or **Keep current draft**, before **Send reply** | The Outlook thread or the Copilot chat, depending on where you sent it | **Envoy** |
