Keep **Angi** for a home-services marketplace, reviews, and a posted request. Use **Envoy** for the contacts on a project, the project details, and outreach from your inbox. You don't have to switch.

Using Envoy does not replace Angi. Many people keep both.

Don't expect Envoy to replace Angi's reviews marketplace or quote inbox.

Angi (formerly Angie's List) is one of the best-known home services marketplaces in the U.S. This page explains which job each product fits.

## Angi vs Envoy

Angi is a home services marketplace for finding a pro for a defined job. Envoy is a project workspace where you choose contacts and email them from Gmail or Microsoft. You can use both.

## At a glance

Each product fits the job it is built for. Read the table as a guide to which tool handles which job — Angi for marketplace discovery and reviews, Envoy for contacts, project details, and email from your inbox.

| | **Envoy** | **Angi** |
|---|-----------|----------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Marketplace connecting homeowners with pros |
| **Who initiates contact** | You choose contacts and reach out | Pros often respond to your posted request |
| **Best for** | Contacts, project details, and outreach across several providers | Finding a pro for a defined home service job |
| **Project context** | Title, location, budget, deadline, and contacts in one place. Coming soon: a planning **Timeline** of milestones and tasks | Job request focused on a single service category |
| **Communication** | Email from your Gmail or Microsoft | Platform-mediated leads and messaging |
| **Envoy assistance** | **Ask Envoy** + draft outreach | Varies by Angi product/experience |
| **Search before signup** | Homepage contact search without an account | Browse the directory without an account. A match or quote request usually needs an account |
| **Pro economics** | Direct relationship; no lead fees to you | Pros may pay for leads through Angi's model |

## What Angi does well

Angi excels when you have a **clear, single-category home need** — a plumber for a leak, an electrician for an outlet, a roofer for a repair, and similar jobs — and you want access to a large pool of reviewed local pros without building a project plan yourself.

Strengths include:

- Broad directory of home service categories
- Established brand recognition in home services
- Reviews and ratings to evaluate pros
- Familiar "find a pro" flow for homeowners

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy is built for the contacts, project details, and outreach that sit alongside a marketplace:

### You pick the contacts

On Envoy's homepage, describe your project and search by location. Review results and check badges like **Onboarded to Envoy**. You're building a shortlist — not posting a job and waiting to see who bites.

<Snippet file="snippets/max-contacts-note.mdx" />

### Your project stays organized

A kitchen remodel isn't one Angi category — it's cabinets, counters, plumbing, electrical, and maybe a GC. Envoy keeps contacts, details, and conversation context attached to **one project**, not scattered across separate requests.

### Outreach comes from you

Envoy drafts emails; you approve and send from **your own inbox**. When a contact replies, the thread is yours. There's no marketplace middle layer deciding who gets your message or when.

### Envoy helps you plan, not just match

**Ask Envoy** helps you think through scope, priorities, and questions before outreach. **Draft with Envoy** lets you tighten a draft before you **Approve & Send**.

## When to use Angi

Choose **Angi** when:

- You need one type of home service pro quickly
- Reviews and Angi's pro network are your primary decision tools
- You prefer a marketplace to route leads to available pros
- The job is straightforward and doesn't require multi-vendor coordination

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- You're planning a project that spans **multiple trades or phases**
- You want to **control who you contact** and what you say to them
- You need project details, contacts, and outreach together
- You want to search for contacts **before committing to an account**
- Keeping conversations in **your email** matters for follow-through

## Coming soon to Envoy

These Envoy features are on the way. They aren't in the app yet, and the page will change when they arrive.

Angi works one job request at a time. These help you plan a job with several pros on one Envoy project.

- **Timeline and tasks:** on **Project**, a planning **Timeline** shows milestones by month (for example, order cabinets or book the electrician) and opens each one into its tasks. You can add a task and give it an owner and a due date.
- **Compare:** under **Contacts**, pick a type of service provider, such as electricians, and check two to four of them to see them side by side. Comparing never sends an email.

## Can you use both?

Yes. Many people use a marketplace like Angi to discover or validate a pro, and Envoy for the contacts, project details, and outreach — especially when the same project involves several contacts and ongoing email follow-up.
