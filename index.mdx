**Plan any project. We line up the contacts.**

Envoy helps you describe what you're planning, discover service provider contacts, keep project details, and send outreach from your own inbox.

<Note>
  Envoy finds service providers (**Contacts**) and helps you email them. A wedding website, registry, guest list and RSVPs, review directories, contracts and invoices, and day-of tools stay in the products you already use for those jobs.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to search for contacts?">
    No. You can describe your project and search for contacts on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before creating an account. Your selections are saved in your browser until you're ready to sign up. After you sign in, you can also search during project creation from **Dashboard**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What are Contacts in Envoy?">
    **Contacts** are service providers — electricians, caterers, contractors, and other pros — that you add to your project or contacts directory. They are not a guest list or a personal address book.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do I need to connect my email?">
    Outreach messages are sent from **your** Gmail or Microsoft account, not from Envoy directly. Connecting your inbox lets Envoy prepare drafts and send approved messages on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time from **Account**.
  </Accordion>

    <Snippet file="snippets/max-contacts-note.mdx" />
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?">
    Use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** on the [Sign in](https://app.hello-envoy.com/login) page. Envoy does not support email-and-password sign-in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm a service provider. Can I join Envoy?">
    Yes. Register as a **Pro** at [Create account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/register?accountType=vendor). See [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro) for what to expect during approval.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
