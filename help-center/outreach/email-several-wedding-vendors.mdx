You can email several wedding vendors from one project. Each vendor gets their own note. Envoy drafts it. You click **Approve & Send**, and it goes from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox you connected. It is not a mass email.

Wedding vendors (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, caterer, photographer, venue, etc. They are not your guest list.

## Before you begin

- An Envoy account
- The vendors you want to email, or a short description and a ZIP or postal code so you can search
- A Gmail or Microsoft inbox you can connect

You can search on the [homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you create an account. This guide starts from **Dashboard**, after you are signed in.

## How do I email several wedding vendors at once?

Put the vendors on one wedding project and finish planning in **Chat**. Envoy prepares a separate draft for each contact. You review each draft and approve it. Each person gets their own email from your inbox.

1. From **Dashboard**, click **+ New project** or **New Project**.
2. On **Essentials**, enter a **Title**, a **Description**, and a **Location**.
3. Put the guest count in the **Description**. Use **Location** for the city or address. You can add the wedding date as the **Deadline** after the project opens.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. On **Contacts**, select the people you want, such as a florist, caterer, photographer, etc.
6. Click **Create project**. If you selected contacts, the button shows **Create project + N contacts**. The project opens on **Overview**.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/projects/project-overview.png" alt="Project Overview tab with details and contacts" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/projects/project-overview-dark.png" alt="Project Overview tab with details and contacts" />

7. On **Overview**, click **Edit**. Add a **Budget** and a **Deadline**, then click **Save**.
8. To add someone who was not in the search, click **+ New contact** and enter a **Name** and **Email**. Or click **+ Attach existing** to add someone already in **Contacts**.
9. Open **Account** and go to **Connected Email Accounts**.
10. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**, then finish signing in. The first inbox you connect is marked **Primary**. Outreach sends from that inbox.
11. Open the project and click **Chat**. Ask what the first note to each contact should include. Press **Send**.
12. When planning is complete, Envoy asks if you have any last details. When you are ready, Envoy prepares a draft for each contact. **Chat** tells you the drafts are ready.
13. Open **Outreach** and expand the first contact. Read that contact's draft. Edit the **Subject** or **Body** if you want.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/outreach/new-message.png" alt="Outreach draft ready to review before you send" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/outreach/new-message-dark.png" alt="Outreach draft ready to review before you send" />

14. Optional: In **Draft with Envoy**, type what to change and click **Go**. Click **Undo** if you want the earlier wording.
15. Click **Approve & Send**. The status changes to **Sent** when the note leaves your inbox.
16. Repeat for the next contact. Approve each note on its own. You still review every send.

A reply shows on that contact's thread and in your inbox. The status **Received** means a reply arrived. See [Reply to contacts](/help-center/outreach/reply-to-contacts).

## How do I get quotes from several caterers without a form on every site?

Add each caterer as a **Contact** on the wedding, then approve a separate note for each one. Envoy writes the draft from the details already on the project. You do not fill out a form on each caterer's website. The quote comes back in their reply.

1. On **Overview**, click **🔍 Find new contacts** and search for caterers. Or click **+ New contact** and enter the **Name** and **Email** of a caterer you already know.
2. Keep the city, guest count, budget, and deadline on the project so each draft can use those details.
3. In **Chat**, ask what a caterer needs in the first note. When you are ready, Envoy prepares a draft for each caterer.
4. Open **Outreach**, read the first caterer's draft, and click **Approve & Send**.
5. Do the same for the next caterer. Each note is its own email.

When a caterer replies, open their thread on **Outreach**. That reply is also in your Gmail or Microsoft inbox. **Received** means their reply arrived. Ask about your date in the note you approve, then read the caterer's answer in their reply.

## Is there a tool that drafts vendor inquiries and sends them from my Gmail?

Yes. Envoy drafts each inquiry from your project details. Connect Gmail under **Connected Email Accounts**, review the draft, and click **Approve & Send**. The message comes from your Gmail address. You can connect Microsoft the same way.

You approve every send. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.
