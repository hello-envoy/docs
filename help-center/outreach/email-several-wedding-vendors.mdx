You can email several wedding vendors from one project. Envoy drafts a note for each one. You click **Send**, and it goes from the Gmail or Microsoft inbox you connected. It is not a mass email.

Each vendor gets their own email, with no group CC and no shared list. That's why you approve each note on its own. Add one personal line per vendor before you click **Send**.

Wedding vendors (called **Contacts** in Envoy) are the florist, caterer, photographer, venue, etc. They are not your guest list.

## Before you begin

- An Envoy account
- The vendors you want to email, or a short description and a ZIP or postal code so you can search
- A Gmail or Microsoft inbox connected in **Account** under **Connected Email Accounts**. Your notes come from the inbox marked **Primary** (the inbox your emails come from). See [Connect your email](/help-center/outreach/connect-your-email).

You can search on the [homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) before you create an account. This guide starts from **Dashboard**, after you are signed in.

## How do I email several wedding vendors at once?

Put the vendors on one wedding project and finish planning in **Chat**. Envoy prepares a separate draft for each contact. You review each draft and send it. Each person gets their own email from your inbox.

1. From **Dashboard**, click **+ New project**.
2. On **Essentials**, enter a **Title**, a **Description**, and a **Location**.
3. Put your wedding date and guest count in the **Description**, such as "Our wedding is June 20, with about 120 guests." Use **Location** for the city or address.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. On **Contacts**, select the people you want, such as a florist, caterer, photographer, etc.
6. Click **Create project**. If you selected contacts, the button shows **Create project + N contacts**. The project opens on **Overview**.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/projects/project-overview.png" alt="Project Overview tab with details and contacts" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/projects/project-overview-dark.png" alt="Project Overview tab with details and contacts" />

7. Optional: On **Overview**, click **Edit**. Add a **Budget** and a **Deadline** (the date you need answers by), then click **Save**.
8. To add someone who was not in the search, click **+ New contact** and enter a **Name** and **Email**. Or click **+ Attach existing** to add someone already in **Contacts**.
9. Click **Chat**. Ask what the first note to each contact should include. Press **Send**.
10. When planning is complete, Envoy asks if you have any last details. When you are ready, Envoy prepares a draft for each contact. **Chat** tells you the drafts are ready.
11. Open **Outreach** and expand the first contact. Read that contact's draft. Make sure the date and guest count are in the note. Edit the subject or message if you want.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/outreach/new-message.png" alt="Outreach draft ready to review before you send" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/outreach/new-message-dark.png" alt="Outreach draft ready to review before you send" />

12. Optional: In **How should Envoy start this thread?**, type what to change and click **Go**. Click **Undo** if you want the earlier wording.
13. Click **Send**. The status changes to **Sent** when the note leaves your inbox.
14. Repeat for the next contact. Approve each note on its own. You still review every send.

Added a vendor after **Chat** finished? On **Outreach**, click **+ New Thread**, choose the contact, and click **Create draft**.

A reply shows on that contact's thread and in your inbox. The status **Received** means a reply arrived. See [Reply to contacts](/help-center/outreach/reply-to-contacts).

## How do I get quotes from several caterers without a form on every site?

Add each caterer as a **Contact** on the wedding, then approve a separate note for each one. Envoy drafts the note from the details already on the project. You do not fill out a form on each caterer's website. The quote comes back in their reply.

1. On **Overview**, click **🔍 Find new contacts** and search for caterers. Or click **+ New contact** and enter the **Name** and **Email** of a caterer you already know.
2. Keep the city, wedding date, guest count, and budget on the project so each draft can use those details.
3. In **Chat**, ask what a caterer needs in the first note. When you are ready, Envoy prepares a draft for each caterer.
4. Open **Outreach**, read the first caterer's draft, and click **Send**.
5. Do the same for the next caterer. Each note is its own email.

When a caterer replies, open their thread on **Outreach**. That reply is also in your Gmail or Microsoft inbox. **Received** means their reply arrived.

Envoy doesn't check availability. Ask "Are you free on [date]?" in your note, and the caterer can answer in their reply.

## Is there a tool that drafts vendor inquiries and sends them from my Gmail?

Yes. Envoy drafts each inquiry from your project details. Connect Gmail under **Connected Email Accounts**, review the draft, and click **Send**. The message comes from your Gmail address. You can connect Microsoft the same way.

You approve every send. Disconnect the inbox anytime from **Account**.
