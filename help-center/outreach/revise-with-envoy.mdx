## What you'll accomplish

Rewrite an outreach draft with Envoy before sending — adjust tone, add details, or change length. You can undo a rewrite and try again.

## Before you start

- An open draft on the **Outreach** tab (after clicking **Create draft**)

## Rewrite a draft

1. With a draft open, use the two-line **Draft with Envoy** bar.

2. Enter instructions. For example:
   - *Tighten the tone*
   - *Add missing project details*
   - *Shorten the message*

3. Open the info control on the bar for guidance on what to ask.

4. Click **Go**. Envoy rewrites the draft.

5. Review the new version. Click **Undo** to restore the previous wording, or enter new instructions and click **Go** again.

6. Click **Approve & Send** when the draft is ready.

## Simple vs Detailed

Use the **Simple** and **Detailed** toggles to control how much project context Envoy includes in the message:

| Style | Best for |
|-------|----------|
| **Simple** | Short, direct introductions |
| **Detailed** | Messages that need full project context (deadline, budget, location) |

You can switch styles before or during revision.

## Undo, cancel, or retry

- **Undo** — Restore the wording from before the latest rewrite
- **Cancel draft** — Remove the draft entirely
- **Retry draft generation** — Start over if the initial draft failed to generate

<Tip>
  Be specific in your instructions. "Mention the March deadline and ask for availability" works better than "make it better."
</Tip>
