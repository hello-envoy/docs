## What you'll learn

The end-to-end flow for using Envoy — from your first search to sending outreach emails.

## The full journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover contacts">
    On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe what you're planning and enter your ZIP or postal code — no account required.

    <Snippet file="snippets/max-contacts-note.mdx" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Continue with your selected contacts to register. Complete consent preferences, then finish setting up your first project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your project">
    Use the two-step wizard — **Essentials** and **Contacts** — to name the project, set a location, and attach the contacts you selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage from your dashboard">
    Return to **Dashboard** anytime to open a wedding, open **Contact book**, or open **Settings** from your photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan with Envoy">
    Open a wedding and click **Ask Envoy**. On a larger screen it is at the bottom of the left side. On a phone, tap the Envoy logo in the top bar. Ask questions, clarify scope, and update details like budget or deadline. When you finish planning, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for contacts on the wedding; you still review and approve every send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send outreach">
    Connect Gmail or Microsoft from **Settings**, then open the wedding's **Contacts** tab and choose **Inbox** to review drafts, attach files, revise with Envoy if needed, and send emails to your contacts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inside a wedding

Every wedding has three tabs. A new wedding opens on **Today**. Open a wedding again and Envoy returns to the tab you last used.

On a phone, **Today**, **Contacts**, and **Project** are one row. **Contacts** opens **Inbox**, **Compare**, and **All** in that row. Open a different wedding and **Contacts** starts on **Inbox**.

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Today** | The wedding name and date. Tasks and emails for the day are not available here yet. |
| **Contacts** | **Inbox** for email, **All** for this wedding's contacts. **Compare** is not available yet. |
| **Project** | The wedding name and date, plus **Edit project details**. A weekly plan is not available here yet. |

**Ask Envoy** is not a tab, and it does not appear on **Dashboard**, **Contact book**, or **Account**. On a larger screen it sits at the bottom of the left side. On a phone, the wedding name is in the top bar, and the Envoy logo there opens **Ask Envoy**.

## Navigation at a glance

Once you're signed in, the left side gives you:

- **Dashboard** — Your home base and recent projects
- **Contact book** — Your directory of service providers
- **Weddings** — Every active wedding
- **Ask Envoy** — On a wedding only. On a phone, use the Envoy logo in the top bar.

Your photo in the top right opens **Settings**, **Help Center**, **Give Feedback** when it is available, and **Logout**.

In dark mode, the Envoy logo at the top left is white.

<Tip>
  You don't have to follow every step in order. Many people search for contacts first, then sign up. Others create an account and find contacts during project creation from **Dashboard**, or add them later from **Contact book**.
</Tip>
