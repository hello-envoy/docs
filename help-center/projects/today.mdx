## What you'll accomplish

Open a wedding on **Today**. A new wedding opens here.

## Open Today

1. Open a wedding from **Dashboard**, or from **Weddings** on the left.
2. A new wedding opens on **Today**. Open that wedding again and Envoy returns to the tab you last used. Click **Today** if another tab is selected.

**Today** shows the wedding name. When the wedding has a deadline, the date shows under the name. On a phone, the wedding name is in the top bar.

Tasks and emails for the day are not available on **Today** yet.

## The wedding tabs

Each wedding has three tabs. On a phone, **Today**, **Contacts**, and **Project** sit in one row under the top bar. Tap **Contacts** and that row opens **Inbox**, **Compare**, and **All**, with **Today** and **Project** beside them.

| Tab | What you can do now |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Today** | See the wedding name and date. A new wedding opens here. |
| **Contacts** | Email from **Inbox**, or manage this wedding's contacts on **All**. **Compare** is not available yet. |
| **Project** | See the wedding name and date, and open **Edit project details**. |

The first time you open **Contacts** on a wedding, **Inbox** is selected. Leave **Contacts** and come back on the same wedding, and Envoy keeps **Inbox**, **Compare**, or **All**. Open a different wedding and **Contacts** starts on **Inbox**.

**Ask Envoy** is not a tab. On a larger screen it sits at the bottom of the left side. On a phone, tap the Envoy logo in the top bar. See [Plan with Envoy](/help-center/ask-envoy/chat-planning).
