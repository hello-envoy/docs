## What you'll accomplish

Open a wedding on **Today**. It is the first tab, and a new wedding opens here.

## Open Today

1. Open a wedding from **Dashboard**, or from **Weddings** on the left.
2. **Today** is already selected. If you are on another tab, click **Today**.

**Today** shows the wedding name. When the wedding has a deadline, the date shows under the name.

Tasks and emails for the day are not available on **Today** yet.

## The wedding tabs

Each wedding has three tabs:

| Tab | What you can do now |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Today** | See the wedding name and date. This tab opens first. |
| **Contacts** | Email from **Inbox**, or manage this wedding's contacts on **All**. **Compare** is not available yet. |
| **Project** | See the wedding name and date, and open **Edit project details**. |

**Ask Envoy** is not a tab. On a wedding, it sits at the bottom of the left side. See [Plan with Envoy](/help-center/ask-envoy/chat-planning).
