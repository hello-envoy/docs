## What you'll accomplish

Use **Ask Envoy** on a wedding to refine plans, update project details, find service providers, and prepare outreach for your contacts.

## Before you start

- An open wedding
- Contacts on the wedding, or a plan to [find](/help-center/ask-envoy/find-contacts) and [add](/help-center/ask-envoy/add-contacts) them with Envoy before it prepares outreach drafts

## Steps

1. Open the wedding. **Ask Envoy** is at the bottom of the left side. It does not appear on **Dashboard**, **Contact book**, or **Account**.

2. Click **Ask Envoy**. If the left side is collapsed, **Ask Envoy** is the circle in that same place.

3. On your first visit, Envoy shows a greeting to help you get started.

4. Type your question or request. Once **Ask Envoy** is open, the field says **Type your message...**.

5. Press **Send**. Envoy responds with suggestions scoped to **this wedding** — using your title, description, location, budget, deadline, and other details.

6. Continue the conversation to explore options, clarify requirements, or plan next steps.

7. Click **Minimize** when you want to close it. **Expand** opens the conversation larger, and **Restore** brings it back.

You can keep typing while waiting for a response — the message field stays active.

Replies may include short lists or tables when Envoy is comparing options.

## What Ask Envoy is (and isn't)

**Ask Envoy is for project planning** — scope, your deadline, the sequence of asks, priorities, and preparation for outreach. It knows your project context. A timeline here means when you need answers and what to ask next. Day-of run-of-show stays in the tools you already use for the event.

**Ask Envoy can also explain how things work.** Ask about deadlines and the order of asks, how contractors quote, what to expect from contacts, or what to prioritize. Envoy answers those questions in the context of your project.

**Ask Envoy is not** a general-purpose assistant. Questions unrelated to your project may get redirected back to project planning.

For a list of things you can ask Envoy to do, see [Ask Envoy actions](/help-center/ask-envoy/actions).

## Update project details

Ask Envoy to save a title, description, budget, deadline, or a ZIP or street address. Saved details show in **Project Details**. A search area is not saved as the location.

See [Update project details](/help-center/ask-envoy/update-project-details).

## When planning is complete

When you finish planning, Envoy can prepare outreach drafts for contacts on the wedding. You still review and approve every send.

After you and Envoy align on the project and what to ask for, the conversation moves to a final check:

1. Envoy asks whether you have any last details to add.
2. If you're ready, Envoy prepares outreach drafts for contacts attached to the wedding.
3. Envoy tells you drafts are ready — open **Contacts**, then **Inbox**, to review, revise, and approve every send.

If you're not ready yet, say so. You can keep gathering details and ask Envoy to prepare drafts later.

If no contacts are attached yet, Envoy explains that you need contacts before drafts can be prepared. [Find contacts with Envoy](/help-center/ask-envoy/find-contacts), or add them on **Contacts** → **All**.

<Note>
  You don't need to click **New message** in **Inbox** if Envoy already prepared drafts — open **Inbox** and review what's there first. You still review and approve every send.
</Note>

## After drafts exist

You can keep using **Ask Envoy** after outreach drafts are ready. Ask Envoy to refresh or write new drafts when your scope, contacts, or details change. If you update the project without asking for new drafts, Envoy may check whether you want them prepared again.

## Tips for good results

- Reference specific details: dates, budget, location, or contacts
- Ask process questions: *How do electricians usually scope a panel upgrade?* The same kind of question works for other service providers.
- Ask for recommendations when you're unsure — saying *I don't know* or *you decide* is fine
- Add contacts before you finish planning so Envoy can prepare drafts. Ask in the conversation, or attach them on **All**. You still review and approve every send.

<Tip>
  Use **Ask Envoy** to clarify what you want to say, then review the drafts on **Inbox**. You still review and approve every send.
</Tip>
