## What you'll accomplish

Add contacts Envoy suggested to this wedding. They show on **Contacts** → **All**.

If you do not have suggestions yet, start with [Find contacts with Envoy](/help-center/ask-envoy/find-contacts). To enter a contact yourself or import a file, see [Add or upload contacts](/help-center/contacts/add-or-upload-contacts).

## Before you start

- An open wedding
- Contact suggestions already in the **Ask Envoy** conversation

## Add the contacts you want

Wait until Envoy has listed suggestions. Then send another message.

1. Open the wedding and click **Ask Envoy**.
2. Name who to add, for example *Add the first two*, or say *Add all of them*.
3. Press **Send**.

Envoy adds those contacts to this wedding. They show on **All**. Envoy tells you who was added.

A short *yes* without names does not add anyone. Ask for a search, such as *Find more florists*, when you want more suggestions instead of an add.

Envoy suggests up to five contacts at a time and marks any that are already on the wedding. Skip the ones already there.

## If a contact is not added

Envoy names a suggestion it could not add. Ask again, or add that contact from **Contact book**. See [Add or upload contacts](/help-center/contacts/add-or-upload-contacts).

If the add does not finish, nothing was changed. Ask again in a moment.

## If you add the wrong contact

Open the wedding, click **Contacts**, then **All**. Click **Edit**, then **Detach** on that row. Confirm **Remove?** with **Yes**, or click **Cancel** to keep the contact on the wedding.
