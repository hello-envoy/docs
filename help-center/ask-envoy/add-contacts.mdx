## What you'll accomplish

Add contacts Envoy suggested in Chat to this project. They show on the project's **Overview** tab.

If you do not have suggestions yet, start with [Find contacts with Envoy](/help-center/ask-envoy/find-contacts). To enter a contact yourself or import a file, see [Add or upload contacts](/help-center/contacts/add-or-upload-contacts).

## Before you start

- An open project
- Contact suggestions already in the **Chat** conversation

## Add the contacts you want

Wait until Envoy has listed suggestions. Then send another message.

1. Open the project and click the **Chat** tab.
2. Name who to add, for example *Add the first two*, or say *Add all of them*.
3. Press **Send**.

Envoy adds those contacts to this project. They show on the **Overview** tab. Envoy tells you who was added.

A short *yes* without names does not add anyone. Ask for a search, such as *Find more florists*, when you want more suggestions instead of an add.

Envoy suggests up to five contacts at a time and marks any that are already on the project. Skip the ones already there.

## If a contact is not added

Envoy names a suggestion it could not add. Ask again, or add that contact from **Contacts**. See [Add or upload contacts](/help-center/contacts/add-or-upload-contacts).

If the add does not finish, nothing was changed. Ask again in a moment.

## If you add the wrong contact

Open the project **Overview** tab, click **Edit** in the **Contacts** section, then **Detach** on that row. Confirm **Remove?** with **Yes**, or click **Cancel** to keep the contact on the project.
