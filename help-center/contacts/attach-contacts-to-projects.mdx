## What you'll accomplish

Attach contacts to a project so they appear in **Outreach** and receive Envoy-drafted emails.

## Before you start

- At least one contact in your directory, or use **Find new contacts** to add one
- An open project

## Attach existing contacts

1. Open the project and go to the **Overview** tab.
2. In the **Contacts** section, click **+ Attach existing**.
3. Search or browse your contacts directory.
4. Select contacts to attach.

Attached contacts appear in the project's contact list and become available in **Outreach → New message**.

The project list groups contacts by type, the same way as your **Contacts** page. Contacts without a type appear under **Other**. A single group stays a flat list, and **Other** stays hidden when it would be the only group.

The list shows 10 contacts at a time. Use the previous, next, and page buttons to move through it. A group continued from the previous page is marked **(continued)**, and its count is the total across every page.

## Add a new contact to the project

1. Click **+ New contact** on the **Overview** tab.
2. Enter **Name** and **Email**.
3. Optionally enter a **Type**, and a **Website** such as `example.com`.
4. Handle any trusted listing match prompt (**Use listing** or **Create separate contact**).
5. The contact is added to both the project and your global directory.

## Find and attach in one step

1. Click **🔍 Find new contacts**.
2. Search by description and location.
3. Select results to add directly to the project.

## Contacts without email

Contacts need email addresses for outreach. If a contact is missing an email, you'll see **Additional contact details required** or outreach will show *Your linked contacts do not have email addresses.*

Add or edit the email on the **Overview** tab before sending outreach.
